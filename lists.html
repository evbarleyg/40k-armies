Four Shadow Legion Lists — one core, four flavors

All four share the core: Be'lakor (390) + Pink Horrors + Nurglings + Legionaries + the Word Bearers paint story. Points are 11th-ed Wahapedia values as of 2026-07-26; totals are ±40 pending app verification (see research.md). Prices reference listings.json.

1 · THE YO-YO COURT — teleport control (closest to owned)

Roster: Be'lakor 390 · Lord of Change 320 · Chaos Lord 90 +Fade to Darkness 30 → Legionaries 90 · 2nd Legionaries 90 · Master of Possession 60 → Possessed ~120 · Havocs ~165 · Fateskimmer 95 · Exalted Flamer 65 → Pink Horrors ~85 · Nurglings 45 ×2 · Flamers 60 · Screamers 70 · Flesh Hounds ~80 · Cultists ~65. ≈ 1,975

Playstyle: Positional attrition. Horrors + Cultists sticky the home objectives, Nurglings screen midboard, Be'lakor anchors center with his shroud while the Lord of Change shoots. The Lord+Legionaries Fade package deep strikes, deletes an objective-holder, vanishes, Rapid-Ingresses free, repeats. Binding Shadow yo-yos whatever else gets in trouble. You win primaries slowly and let opponents exhaust themselves on things that split, regenerate, or leave.

In/Out vs collection: Everything owned is in except War Dogs and Blue-Horrors-as-unit. Out: War Dogs (no room), Screamers optional vs 2nd Nurglings.

To buy: Chaos Lord (~$20–30), Flesh Hounds ×5 (~$20–50), Cultists ×10 (~$28). ~$70–110 total.

2 · THE CRIMSON SPEARHEAD — Khorne pressure

Roster: Be'lakor 390 · Bloodcrushers 135 ×2 · Skulltaker 85 +Leaping Shadows 25 → Bloodletters ~105 · Bloodmaster 65 → Bloodletters ~105 · Karanak 70 → Flesh Hounds ~80 · 2nd Flesh Hounds ~80 · Chaos Lord 90 +Fade 30 → Legionaries 90 · Warp Talons(6) 130 · Nurglings 45 ×2 · Cultists ~65 · Pink Horrors ~85. ≈ 1,990

Playstyle: Advance-and-charge alpha strike. Everything Khorne moves fast and charges out of Advance; scouting Skulltaker brick and Bloodcrushers threaten turn-1/2 charges; Channeled Wrath (+1 AP Lance) is your every-turn stratagem into hard targets; hounds handle actions/backfield with their free uppy-downy. Kill fast, snowball, never give the opponent a quiet turn. Highest skill ceiling of the four — charge sequencing decides everything.

In/Out: Out: Lord of Change, Fateskimmer, Havocs, Possessed+MoP, War Dogs, Flamers/Screamers. In from photos: your Bloodletters.

To buy: Bloodcrushers ×6 (~$48–105/3), 2nd Bloodletters ×10 (via Skulltaker+letters lot $25), Skulltaker (~$25), Karanak (~$30), Flesh Hounds ×10 ($86 9-pack + single), Warp Talons ($44, or the $107 Word Bearers-scheme squad), Cultists ($28). ~$250–330 total.

3 · THE FESTERING COURT — Nurgle attrition (cheapest to field: ~$20)

Roster: Be'lakor 390 · Lord of Change 320 · Beasts of Nurgle 75 ×3 · Plague Drones 110 · Nurglings 45 ×2 · Pink Horrors ~85 · Havocs ~165 · Legionaries ~90 ×2 · MoP 60 → Possessed ~120 · War Dog Karnivore ~155 ×2 (Dreadblades). ≈ 2,050 — trim one Beast to fit.

Playstyle: The unmoving feast. Beasts (−1 wound vs high-S in your detachment!) and Drones squat objectives and simply do not die; the Gloam Rot benefit makes them absurd into anti-tank weapons. War Dogs and Havocs provide the shooting the daemon half lacks; Be'lakor walks wherever the fight is thickest. You score by existing. This list uses the photo-extras the seller's list didn't even count — you own almost all of it already.

In/Out: Out: Fateskimmer, Exalted Flamer, Flamers, Screamers. In: everything Nurgle from the photos.

To buy: one more Nurglings box (~$20). That's it. Verify War Dog Dreadblades legality in-app.

4 · WORD BEARERS ASCENDANT — the mortal half rises

Roster: Be'lakor 390 · Chaos Lord 90 +Fade 30 → Legionaries ~90 · Termie Lord 85 → Chaos Terminators 145 · Chosen ~90 · Warp Talons(6) 130 · MoP 60 → Possessed ~120 · Havocs ~165 · Cultists ~65 ×2 · Nurglings 45 · Pink Horrors ~85 · Flamers 60 · Screamers 70 · Fateskimmer 95 +Leaping Shadows 25. ≈ 1,940

Playstyle: The whole marine wing deep strikes (Disciples of Be'lakor). This is a reserves shell game: half your army starts off-board, and every turn 2–3 arrivals threaten simultaneous charges everywhere. Dark Pacts on every marine unit (lethal or sustained hits each activation) makes even Legionaries punch up. Thematically maximal — the daemons are the garnish, the Word Bearers are the sermon. Weakest into gunlines that castle; strongest into armies that spread out.

In/Out: Out: Lord of Change, War Dogs, Exalted Flamer. Terminators: photos suggest you may own them — verify against the box when it arrives.

To buy: Chaos Lord ($20–30), Chosen ×5 (~$40 street), Warp Talons ($44 or $107 WB-scheme), Cultists ×20 ($28+$45), possibly Terminators. ~$130–250.

5 · THE FADETHIRSTER — competitive core (NEW)

Shadow Legion (2 DP, Purge the Foe) + Cavalcade of Chaos (1 DP). This is the tournament shape — every 4-1 Shadow Legion result on Listhammer since 11th launch runs this skeleton. Reference: Marney, Edinburgh Super Major 4-1; Kersley, Burn & Learn 2nd.

Playstyle: Two independent kill-loops. The Skullmaster + 6 Bloodcrushers Scout 9" → Advance → charge T1 (Murderer's Cowl), Channelled Wrath for Lance/+1 AP, Rendmaster aura for +1 to wound. The Bloodthirster ("Fadethirster") drops T2, one-shots a vehicle (7A S16 AP-4 D6+2), Fades to Darkness into Reserves at end of Fight, Rapid Ingress back during opponent's Movement, repeat — it's off the board between swings. Nurglings/Cultists/Horrors hold home; Flesh Hounds and Plague Drones do secondaries.

In/Out vs collection: Out: LoC, Fateskimmer, Screamers, Flamers, MoP+Possessed, War Dogs, Blue Horrors. In from photos: Bloodletters, Plague Drones.

6 · TRIPLE MONSTER — Be'lakor + Bloodthirster + Lord of Change (NEW)

Shadow Legion (2 DP, Purge the Foe). The most beginner-forgiving list: three durable centrepieces, minimal reserves juggling.

Playstyle: Three T10–11 4++ monsters each project a 6" Greater Daemon Shadow-of-Chaos aura — spread them out and most of the board is in your Shadow by T2. Bloodthirster runs the Fade loop; LoC shoots and buffs Tzeentch (Stealth on all your Horrors/Screamers/Flamers via Penumbral Puppetry); Be'lakor bullies mid under Wreathed in Shadows. Blue Horrors Infiltrate onto a forward objective. Death Denied every Command phase on whichever monster took the most fire. Weakest on secondary scoring — if you find you're losing on cards, cut the LoC for a Skullmaster+Bloodcrushers package and you're playing List 5.

In/Out vs collection: Uses almost everything owned. Out: Exalted Flamer, Flamers, 2nd Legionaries, War Dogs.
