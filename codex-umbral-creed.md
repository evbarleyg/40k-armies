## PART I — THE UMBRAL CREED

The Word Bearers taught the galaxy that faith is a weapon. The Umbral Creed is
what happens when a chapter of that Legion reads its own scripture too closely.

They were a preaching company like ten thousand others: crimson armor, silver
trim, portable reliquaries, a schedule. They burned what they were told to burn
and illuminated the margins of the Book of Lorgar by lamplight afterward. The
company's Master of Possessions, a patient man named Sor Vekh, noticed the same
defect in every summoning he performed: the daemon arrives, the daemon obeys,
and behind the daemon's eyes something older watches the transaction and finds
it funny.

Vekh did what scholars do. He footnoted it.

Forty years of footnotes became a concordance, and the concordance became a
heresy within the heresy: the four gods are loud, and the thing beneath them is
not. Prayer addressed to the silence gets answered more precisely. The company
tested this the way the Word Bearers test everything, on a world that could not
refuse, and the answer that came back had wings, a blade, and the oldest name in
the ledger of the damned.

Be'lakor did not demand worship. He corrected their pronunciation.

They repainted nothing. The crimson stays, because the Creed holds that they are
still Word Bearers, still faithful, still reading the same book. They have simply
reached a later chapter. What changed is the congregation: horrors that chant in
colors, hounds that answer the tithe, nurglings that annotate the footnotes by
chewing them, and above the whole procession the First Prince, who finds
everything the gods built derivative and says so.

**The Creed's doctrine, in one line:** the gods are quotations. Serve the source.

> *On the 27th day the Word arrived and none were home to receive it.*
> *It waited with a neighbor. This too is recorded.*
>
> — Remembrancer's marginalia, Ledger of Tithes

### The Blood Tithe

A congregation that worships silence still has to pay its bills, and the coin of
Chaos is blood. In the high summer of the Creed's second year the tithe came due,
and the court of Khorne arrived to collect it in person: a Bloodthirster, unbound
and unassembled, patient in his crate; a Herald with ten sworn killers; five
hounds of flesh who can hear a lie at nine inches and take it personally.

The Creed does not love Khorne. The Creed pays him, promptly, the way you pay
any collector you intend to keep using. Khorne does not care from whence the
blood flows. Lately it has flowed mostly from California.

## PART II — ARMOURY OF SHADOW

*The two rules engines the whole army runs on. Condensed from
`belakor-shadow-legion-guide.md`; that file holds the full detail.*

### Shadow of Chaos (army rule)

The battlefield divides into *in shadow* and *not*. Your zone starts in shadow;
it spreads with map control (hold at least half the objectives in the enemy
deployment zone at the start of a phase and their zone joins yours for that
phase), and Be'lakor and the Greater Daemons carry it with them in a 6" bubble.

- **Daemonic Manifestation** — friendly daemons in shadow add 1 to Battle-shock
  tests; a passed test heals D3 wounds, or returns up to D3 destroyed models to
  a Battleline unit. The Horrors are the reason this line matters.
- **Daemonic Terror** — enemy units in shadow, or near a Greater Daemon,
  subtract 1 from Battle-shock tests and take D3 mortal wounds on a failure.
  Battle-shocked units drop to OC 0 and stop holding objectives.

The army's true win condition: push the shadow forward, make your holders
unkillable, make theirs stop counting.

### Shadow Legion (detachment — 2 DP, Purge the Foe)

Be'lakor's own legion: every god's daemons plus mortal Chaos Space Marines under
one banner. Be'lakor and all Heretic Astartes gain SHADOW LEGION and UNDIVIDED;
all Legiones Daemonica gain SHADOW LEGION.

| Allegiance | Boon |
|---|---|
| Khorne | Advance, shoot **and charge** |
| Tzeentch | Attackers take −1 to hit |
| Nurgle | −1 to wound when attack Strength beats Toughness |
| Slaanesh | Cannot be Overwatched |
| Undivided (incl. CSM) | Gain Dark Pacts; Be'lakor auto-passes its test |

**The mortal half.** Up to half the army from a fixed CSM allowlist: Chaos Lord
(foot, Terminator, jump pack), Sorcerer (foot, Terminator), Master of
Possessions, Dark Apostle, Legionaries, Chosen, Possessed, Raptors, Warp Talons,
Havocs, and Damned units. Heretic Astartes here gain Deep Strike.

**The fine print.** No vehicles (the War Dogs attend as Chaos Knights allies,
outside the detachment). No Daemon Princes. No Epic Heroes except Be'lakor.

**Known toolkit.** *Warp Surge* (1 CP: a daemon unit in shadow charges after
Advancing), *Denizens of the Warp* (1 CP: deep strike at 3" instead of 9"),
*Leaping Shadows* (enhancement: the bearer's unit gets Scouts 9" — the reviews'
pick, and the reason the Bloodthirster entry below says what it says).

## PART III — THE HOSTS OF THE CREED

*Unit entries in codex order. Points from the seller's 11th-edition list where
known; new arrivals carry a dash until verified in the app.*

### The Dark Master

**BE'LAKOR — The Source** *(390 pts, painted)*

First of the Daemon Princes, author of the silence the Creed reads. On the table
he is a moving cathedral: tough (T11, 3+, 20 wounds, invulnerable save, −1 to be
hit, no re-rolls against him, untargetable at long range), decently violent
(Devastating Wounds on the strike, Sustained Hits on the sweep), and carrying
three auras that are the actual purchase: the shadow itself, re-roll 1s to hit
for the faithful, and a 9" terror bubble that heals him D3 every time an enemy
unit fails its nerve nearby.

*On the battlefield:* he is the umbrella, and everything fragile stands under
him. Fly him behind terrain turns one and two, or deep strike him with the
second wave. His damage is real but his job is position: wherever he stands, the
army rule follows and the enemy's Leadership starts costing them mortal wounds.
Do not spend him like an assassin; spend him like weather.

> *He was first. Every shadow since is quotation.*
>
> — The Anagnost, Sermons in Reverse

### The First Choir (Tzeentch)

**LORD OF CHANGE — The Anagnost** *(300 pts, painted)*

The Creed's reader-aloud, who performs the silence in nine voices. The second
Greater Daemon, the second terror aura, the second 6" shadow bubble, and the
army's only real magic and shooting platform.

*On the battlefield:* the other half of the double arrival. Land him and
Be'lakor together, turn two or three, on the flank with the fewest guns. Two
flying monsters at once is a question most opponents can answer only once.

**FATESKIMMER** *(95 pts, painted)* — a Herald on a Burning Chariot, fast enough
to drag the −1-to-hit boon and a shadow's edge wherever the line is thinnest.
Screens for the monsters; harasses; dies loudly if ignored, which is the point.

**EXALTED FLAMER** *(65 pts, painted)* — mobile fire support with the Tzeentch
boon. Parks near the Horrors and punishes anything that comes to clear them.

**PINK HORRORS ×10** *(150 pts, painted)* — the congregation. Battleline, which
in this army means the Manifestation rule resurrects them D3 at a time while
they hold the midfield and giggle. Split into Blues, then Brimstones, as they
die: one purchase, three waves of bodies.

**BLUE HORRORS ×10 / BRIMSTONES ×9** *(125 pts as alternates, painted)* — the
second and third verses of the same hymn.

**SCREAMERS ×3** *(80 pts, painted)* — fast movement-phase damage for secondaries
and rear objectives. They do not fight so much as fly through and apologize.

### The Blood Tithe (Khorne) — *new musters, August 2026*

**EXALTED BLOODTHIRSTER — The Unbound Word** *(pts: verify; unassembled,
unpainted)*

The collector himself. He arrived in pieces, which the Creed regards as
theologically correct: wrath, some assembly required. He is also the host's one
true hobby project — sprues, glue, and a red that will have to be earned.

*On the battlefield (once mustered):* the detachment's most violent opening.
Give him *Leaping Shadows* and he Scouts 9" before the first turn, then uses the
Khorne boon to Advance and still charge. The reviews call the Scouting
Bloodthirster the detachment's best trick; your copy just needs arms first.

*On the workbench:* the seller ran late, apologized, and enclosed a small gift
for the delay. The Creed does not forgive. The Creed left five stars regardless,
for the packaging was honest.

**BLOODMASTER & BLOODLETTERS ×10 — The Tithe-Takers** *(pts: verify; painted)*

The melee hammer the strategy doc said the army lacked, bought at auction for
less than the doc budgeted. A Herald of Khorne and ten hellblades that hit like
a legal judgment.

*On the battlefield:* deep strike or advance under Warp Surge; with the Khorne
boon they Advance and charge natively, so every turn they are alive they are a
threat everywhere within eighteen inches or so. Point them at whatever is
holding an objective, not whatever is scariest. They are here to take ground,
and the mortal wounds from Terror finish what the hellblades start.

**FLESH HOUNDS ×5 — The Nine-Inch Truth** *(pts: verify; well painted)*

Collared in brass, allergic to sorcery, fast.

*On the battlefield:* the army's missing speed, partially found. Advance-and-
charge cavalry that grabs far objectives, hunts backfield characters, and
screens the monsters' landing zones. Their anti-psyker collars are a dry joke in
an army this magical; the hounds have not noticed.

**BLOODCRUSHERS — inbound** *(ETA Aug 18 – Sep 1; "some damaged," per the
listing, which for Khorne is practically a blessing)*

Juggernaut cavalry. Repair, re-base, and they become the Tithe's armored fist —
note the July 2026 balance pass already taxed them 10 points for being good.

**KHORNE DAEMONS ARMY LOT — inbound** *(ETA Aug 10–17; contents to be
catalogued on arrival)*

A painted lot bought on a $179 offer. The Ledger will be amended when the crate
opens; the Creed expects letters, and suspects a Herald.

### The Margins (Nurgle)

**NURGLINGS ×3 BASES — The Annotators** *(45 pts, painted)*

They chew the footnotes. Infiltrating objective-sitters who start the midfield
shadow economy on turn one and are, point for point, the most load-bearing
comedians in the army.

*On the battlefield:* deploy them on the objectives you intend to keep. With the
Nurgle boon they shrug high-Strength fire, and every phase they survive is a
phase the enemy spent shooting at giggles.

### The Bearers of the Word (Heretic Astartes)

**CHAOS LORD — Obsidius Mallex, in service as the Creed's Castellan** *($20 of
Blackstone Fortress plastic; runs as a standard Chaos Lord — the Epic Hero ban
does not apply because the datasheet is the generic one)*

*On the battlefield:* the CSM half's missing officer. Attach to Legionaries or
Chosen for re-rolls and a Dark Pacts babysitter; with the detachment he deep
strikes with his unit.

**MASTER OF POSSESSIONS — Sor Vekh, First Footnotist** *(60 pts, painted)*

The scholar who started all this, still taking notes.

*On the battlefield:* glue him to the Possessed. Buffs, protects, and makes the
bomb worth its crater.

**POSSESSED ×5 — The Later Chapters** *(120 pts, painted)*

What careful reading does to a boarding squad.

*On the battlefield:* the deep-striking melee bomb. Arrive with the monsters,
Warp Surge if the charge needs help, and let Vekh handle the paperwork.

**LEGIONARIES ×10 — The Preaching Company** *(180 pts as 1–2 units, painted)*

The company that footnoted its way here, still crimson, still on schedule.

*On the battlefield:* actions, secondaries, midfield trades, Dark Pacts on
demand. Run as two fives; five brothers is enough for a sermon.

**HAVOCS — The Rearguard Chorus** *(125 pts, 2 lascannon / 2 autocannon,
painted)*

*On the battlefield:* the home objective and the only long guns the detachment
allows. They have held the home line through every engagement so far and have
opinions about the missions where nothing came to them. They recite them.

### Oath-Bound Engines (Chaos Knights allies)

**WAR DOG KARNIVORES ×2 — The Iron Cantors** *(310 pts, painted)*

Not of the detachment — the Shadow Legion admits no vehicles — but the Creed
keeps mercenaries the way it keeps footnotes: outside the main text, doing the
real work. Verify the ally rule before tournaments; kitchen tables will not ask.

*On the battlefield:* fast anti-tank the daemons cannot otherwise buy, and far-
objective muscle while the tithe walks.

## PART IV — WAYS OF WAR

Three doctrines, one army. Pick by opponent.

**The Long Shadow** *(vs. shooting armies).* Start thin: Nurglings on the
midfield objectives, Havocs home, Horrors behind terrain, everything else in
reserve as the rules allow (check current reserve caps before writing it on a
list). Turns one and two the opponent shoots giggles and terrain. Turns two and
three the sky opens: Be'lakor, the Anagnost, and the Possessed arrive together
on the weak flank, Denizens of the Warp tightens a charge if one must land, and
the shadow arrives with them. You were never not there; you were just late on
purpose.

**The Crimson Tithe** *(vs. armies that want time).* The new wing inverts the
tempo. Leaping Shadows on the Bloodthirster, hounds and letters forward,
everything Khorne Advancing and still charging. The tithe hits the midfield on
turn one or two before a castle finishes forming, and the rest of the codex
walks in behind it. Riskier, louder, and the reason the Blood Tithe was
purchased.

**The Patient Litany** *(vs. melee armies).* Castle. Horrors screen, monsters
counterpunch, the Manifestation engine resurrects the line while Terror and
Be'lakor's aura tax every Leadership check the opponent makes near an objective.
Battle-shock the contesters, never the champions; a flipped objective at the
right moment beats a dead Terminator squad.
