# design/

Working material for the Muster console's design decisions — kept in the repo on purpose (`SPEC-muster.md` §6:
"Keep the concept sketches in the repo").

- `concepts/` — the three home-screen + navigation concepts from the design tournament (A four-questions-first,
  B table-first, C codex-first), as real pages over `ledger.css`. Numbers in them are illustrative.
- `shots/` — their screenshots at 390 px dark and 1280 px light, as judged.
- `battery/` — (git-ignored) screenshots produced by `tools/shoot.js` for each review-battery round.

The judges' scores, the grafts adopted and the risks they named are in `DECISIONS.md` §4; the battery findings in §5.
