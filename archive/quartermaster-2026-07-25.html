What 11th edition changed (verified)

97-agent deep-research run, three-vote adversarial verification per claim. Points current to Munitorum Field Manual v1.1 (22 Jul 2026). Rules quoted from Wahapedia/40k.app cross-checks — confirm in the official app before tournament play.

Cover is now −1 to hit
Not a save bonus — so your invuln-save daemons finally benefit. Cited as the biggest army-wide Daemons buff of the edition.
First Prince of Chaos (your detachment rule)
Khorne: shoot & charge after Advance · Tzeentch: Stealth + −1 to be hit in melee · Nurgle: −1 to wound vs S>T · Slaanesh: immune to snap shooting · your marines: Dark Pacts + Deep Strike.
Master of Possession: legal
Both rules databases list him in Shadow Legion's allowed units. Your seller built a legal army.
The Fade loop survives
Fade to Darkness (30) and Leaping Shadows (25) unchanged. Shade Path nerfed to −1" charge. New limit: one stratagem per unit per phase.
Hidden / Gone to Ground
Infantry in terrain that hold fire can only be detected within 15" (12" if obscured) — your Nurglings and Cultists got sneakier.
July 22 points
Lord of Change 300→320 · Bloodcrushers +10 (6-pack) · Kairos +10 · Shalaxi −25. Daemons "slight loser," Shadow Legion still rated strong.
