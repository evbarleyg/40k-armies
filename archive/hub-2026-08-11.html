The army today

One painted eBay lot (236942163636, $730, arrived late July): Be'lakor, a Lord of Change and the Tzeentch court (Fateskimmer, Exalted Flamer, Pink/Blue/Brimstone Horrors, Flamers, Screamers), Nurglings plus photo-extra Beasts of Nurgle, Plague Drones and ~10 Bloodletters, a Word Bearers half (Master of Possession, Possessed, Legionaries, Havocs, maybe Terminators) and two War Dog Karnivores — ≈1,985 playable points.

  • Playable now: List B (Festering Court) if the photo-extras check out — nothing to buy but a Nurgling box. List F (Triple Monster) and E (Fadethirster) wait on one Bloodthirster; A and C on a Chaos Lord, Cultists and Flesh Hounds.
  • Settled rules: Chaos Lord and Master of Possession are legal in Shadow Legion; Skulltaker/Karanak are not (Epic Heroes); the Bloodthirster is one 320-pt datasheet and may take Fade to Darkness; War Dogs ride along via Dreadblades (≤3); Be'lakor is always the Warlord. Re-verify after any dataslate.
