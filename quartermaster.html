Buying

The dashboards used to carry live listings here; they were July prices and every one has ended, so they moved to the archive (scout report — graded picks per unit and the cost to finish each list on that day). What lasts:

  • Cost to finish, order of magnitude — B ≈ $40 (a Nurglings box), F ≈ $110–190 and E ≈ $390–490 (both hinge on one Bloodthirster, NIB ≈ $143, open-box auctions ≈ $85), A ≈ $90–120 (Chaos Lord + Flesh Hounds + Cultists), C ≈ $160–215, D ≈ $480–545.
  • Where the value is — daemon kits are shared with Age of Sigmar, so "Blades of Khorne" / "Disciples of Tzeentch" listings are the same models and often cheaper; painted single characters (Chaos Lord, heralds) go for $20–30; matched liquidation clusters from one seller combine shipping.
  • How to look — read-only, photos not titles, a bid is not a price: tools/ebay_search.js and tools/ebay_fetch.js from a session that can reach eBay (method note). Etsy blocks automation — search it by hand.

Key Rules Verified

Cross-checked Wahapedia 11e + Tabletop Battles + Listhammer, 2026-07-27. Full citations in the Primer, Appendix A.

  • Chaos Lord & Master of Possession are legal in Shadow Legion — both explicitly named on the Thralls of the First Prince allowed-units list. Wahapedia
  • Skulltaker & Karanak are ILLEGAL — both carry the EPIC HERO keyword and Thralls bans all Epic Heroes except Be'lakor. Old List 2 was invalid; List D replaces them with Skullmaster + Bloodmaster. Wahapedia
  • Bloodthirster is 320 pts (not ~165), single unified datasheet, and can take Fade to Darkness — it's a SHADOW LEGION Character, not Epic Hero, and core enhancement rules have no Monster exclusion. Wahapedia
  • War Dogs via Dreadblades: max 3 in any all-CHAOS army (no Warlord, no enhancements). Your two Karnivores are legal in List B. Wahapedia CK
  • Be'lakor must be Warlord if present — his Supreme Commander ability is mandatory, not optional. Wahapedia
