I · The Army

How the army works

The Shadow of Chaos
Your deployment zone is always in shadow; No Man's Land joins it in any phase where you hold at least half of its objectives, and the enemy zone joins when you hold half of theirs. Daemonic Manifestation: a daemon unit in shadow adds 1 to its Battle-shock roll and, on a pass, heals D3 or, if Battleline, returns up to D3 destroyed non-Character models. Daemonic Terror: an enemy unit in shadow, or within 6" of a Greater Daemon, tests Battle-shock at -1 and takes D3 mortal wounds when it fails.
Warp Rifts
A daemon deep-striking wholly inside the shadow, or wholly within 6" of a friendly Greater Daemon of its own god, can be set up more than 6" from the enemy instead of 9".
Dark Pacts
When a unit with it shoots or fights it may take a Leadership test: fail and it suffers D3 mortal wounds, and either way its weapons gain Lethal Hits or Sustained Hits 1 for the phase. Here Be'lakor and every Heretic Astartes unit have it; Be'lakor passes automatically.

I · The Army · Shadow Legion · 2 DP

The detachment

Every unit carries its god's boon as a datasheet ability. Khorne, Murderer's Cowl: Advance and still shoot and charge. Tzeentch, Penumbral Puppetry: Stealth and -1 to be hit in melee. Nurgle, Gloam Rot: -1 to wound when the attack's Strength beats Toughness. Slaanesh, as recorded in July: cannot be Overwatched. Undivided, Disciples of Be'lakor: Dark Pacts, and Deep Strike for Heretic Astartes. Be'lakor must be the Warlord; no Daemon Princes, no vehicles, no Epic Heroes but him; up to half the army from the Thralls of the First Prince allowlist.

Role

Warlord, umbrella and shadow engine

How to play it

Round one, Wreathed in Shadows: nothing within 6" of him can be shot from beyond 18", so castle the Horrors, Flamers and Legionaries under his wings and make the enemy walk into the shadow to shoot. Once the fighting starts, Shadow Lord for re-roll 1s across the castle or the strike wave. The round after you have damaged units sitting near objectives, Pall of Despair: forced tests at -1 inside the shadow, D3 mortal wounds per failure, and he heals D3 per failure. He is a fighter second. The strike profile (7 attacks, S14, AP-4, D6+1, Devastating Wounds) kills a Character or a light tank; the sweep (14 attacks, S8, AP-3, Sustained Hits 1) clears infantry. Keep him at 7 or more wounds where you can; at 6 he hits at -1.

Pairs with

Legionaries and Possessed, which deep-strike beside him and Dark Pact inside his re-roll aura; the Horrors, whose Manifestation only works inside a shadow; a second monster on the other flank so two bubbles cover the midfield.

Beware

Anti-monster shooting inside 18": a 4+ invulnerable and a 3+ save do not survive massed AP-2. Chaff that ties him up away from objectives. He is an Epic Hero, so no enhancement.

In lists A, B, C, D, E, F, G.
