I · Primer

A turn, and an attack

The five phases

  1. Command. Gain 1 CP. Units below half strength take Battle-shock tests. From round two, score the primary here.
  2. Movement. Each unit makes a Normal move, Advances, Falls Back or stays put. Reinforcements arrive at the end of the phase.
  3. Shooting. Each unit shoots; only Pistols fire from inside engagement range. Overwatch is the opponent's reply, in your turn.
  4. Charge. Declare targets and roll 2D6. Reach engagement range with every target or stay put. Heroic Intervention is the reply.
  5. Fight. Units that charged fight first, then the players alternate. Pile in 3 inches, attack, consolidate 3.

What this army does to the dice

  • +1 to hit in melee for Khorne daemons within 6 inches of a Bloodthirster.
  • Re-roll hit rolls of 1 for everything within 6 inches of Be'lakor under Shadow Lord.
  • +1 to wound for a brick led by a Bloodmaster.
  • Lethal Hits or Sustained Hits 1 for Be'lakor and every marine unit through Dark Pacts.
  • -1 to be hit in melee and Stealth for every Tzeentch daemon; -1 to be wounded by big guns for every Nurgle daemon.
  • +1 Strength, AP and Damage for every Khorne daemon into the Rendmaster's chosen target.

One attack, four rolls

  1. Hit. Roll Attacks dice against BS or WS. An unmodified 6 is a critical hit: Lethal Hits and Sustained Hits key off it.
  2. Wound. Strength against Toughness, per the table. An unmodified 6 is a critical wound: Devastating Wounds keys off it.
  3. Save. The defender rolls its armour save minus the AP, or its invulnerable save, whichever is better.
  4. Damage. Each failed save removes the weapon's Damage from one model. Excess is lost. Mortal wounds skip steps one to three.

I · Primer

How you win

On points, not kills. A massacre that forgot the markers is a loss.

  • The primary: at the start of your Command phase from round two, score for each objective marker you control, meaning more Objective Control within 3 inches than the opponent has. OC 0 never counts.
  • The secondaries: the extra goals drawn or chosen each game. Actions score them, and a unit doing an action does not shoot or charge. The Legionaries and Cultists are the action units.
  • Purge the Foe: this detachment's missions also reward destroying more units than you lose in a round. A screen that dies for nothing hands the opponent that point.
  • This army's engine: push the shadow forward by holding half of No Man's Land, keep Battleline daemons on markers where they regrow, and force Battle-shock tests on whoever contests, so they drop to OC 0 and bleed mortal wounds. Battle-shock the contester, never the champion.
  • The rhythm: turn one, screen and spread. Turns two and three, commit the killers where they both kill and claim. Turns four and five, a live monster on a marker beats any kill.
  • Mission packs change with the season. Read the current one before a game; these are the parts that have held.

invulnerable save A save that AP cannot modify. Use it whenever it is better than the modified armour save. Most of this army has a 4+ or 5+ and no armour to speak of.

Weapon profile

Range How far the weapon reaches, measured base to base. Melee means it is used only in engagement range.
Attacks How many dice the weapon rolls to hit. D6 or D3 means roll for the number each time it fires.
Ballistic Skill The to-hit roll for shooting. N/A means the weapon hits automatically (a Torrent weapon).
Weapon Skill The to-hit roll for melee.
Strength Compared with the target's Toughness to set the wound roll: double it or more wounds on 2+, higher on 3+, equal on 4+, lower on 5+, half or less on 6+.
Armour Penetration Subtracted from the target's armour save: AP-2 turns a 3+ into a 5+. It never touches an invulnerable save.
Damage Wounds removed from one model per unsaved hit. Damage beyond that model's remaining wounds is lost, which is why one-wound daemons do not care about D2.

Weapon abilities

Assault The weapon can be fired in a turn the unit Advanced.
Heavy +1 to hit if the unit did not move this turn.
Pistol Can be fired while in engagement range, at an enemy unit the bearer is engaged with. A model with a pistol and another gun fires one or the other.
Rapid Fire Rapid Fire X: the weapon gains X extra attacks against targets within half its range.
Torrent Hits automatically, no hit roll. That is why Torrent weapons are the ones worth Overwatching with, since Overwatch otherwise only hits on 6s.
Blast +1 attack per five models in the target unit, and it cannot be fired at a unit within engagement range of your own models.
Ignores Cover The target gets no benefit of cover against the attack.
Lethal Hits A critical hit (an unmodified 6 to hit) wounds automatically, skipping the wound roll.
Sustained Hits Sustained Hits X: a critical hit scores X extra hits on top of itself.
Devastating Wounds A critical wound (an unmodified 6 to wound) inflicts its damage as mortal wounds: no armour save, no invulnerable save.
Precision When the target is a unit with a Character attached, the attacker may put the hit on the Character instead of the bodyguard.
Anti-Monster Anti-X Y+: against a target with keyword X, an unmodified wound roll of Y or more counts as a critical wound. With Devastating Wounds that means mortal wounds; the Screamers' bites carry Anti-Monster and Anti-Vehicle 4+.
Lance +1 to wound in a turn the unit charged. The Juggernauts' horns.
Twin-linked Re-roll the wound roll.
Extra Attacks The weapon's attacks are made in addition to the model's other melee weapon, not instead of it.
Hazardous After the weapon is used, roll a D6 for each Hazardous weapon fired: on a 1 the bearer suffers 3 mortal wounds, or is destroyed if it has one wound. The focused witchfire profiles are Hazardous; the base profiles are not.
Psychic The attack counts as psychic. That matters for Anti-Psyker weapons, the Flesh Hounds' collars, and any Feel No Pain that names psychic attacks.
Indirect Fire Can target a unit the bearer cannot see, at -1 to hit and with the target counting as in cover.
Melta Melta X: +X damage against a target within half range.
critical hit An unmodified 6 on the hit roll (critical hit) or the wound roll (critical wound). The trigger for Lethal Hits, Sustained Hits, Devastating Wounds and Anti-X.

Glossary II · abilities and keywords

Core abilities

Deep Strike The unit may start in Reserves and, from the second battle round, arrive at the end of your Movement phase anywhere more than 9 inches from enemy models. Warp Rifts trims that to 6 inches for daemons arriving inside the shadow or beside their own god's Greater Daemon. The detachment hands Deep Strike to the marines too.
Scouts Scouts X inches: before the first turn the unit may move up to X inches, ending more than 9 inches from the enemy. Leaping Shadows grants Scouts 9.
Infiltrators The unit may be deployed anywhere on the table more than 9 inches from the enemy deployment zone and from enemy models. The Nurglings' whole job.
Stealth Enemy ranged attacks against the unit hit at -1. The Tzeentch boon gives it to every Tzeentch daemon here.
Feel No Pain Feel No Pain X+: each time the model would lose a wound, roll a D6; on X or more the wound is not lost. It works against mortal wounds too unless the ability says otherwise.
Deadly Demise Deadly Demise X: when the model is destroyed, roll a D6; on a 6 every unit within 6 inches suffers X mortal wounds. The monsters all have it.
Fights First The unit fights in the Fight phase before units without it, even when the enemy charged.
Leader A Character with Leader may be attached to one of its listed units before the battle. They move and fight as one unit, and the Character cannot be shot out of it (except by Precision) while bodyguards remain.
Aura An ability that affects every eligible unit within its range, the bearer's own unit included. Be'lakor's three Shadow Forms and the Greater Daemons' bubbles are auras.
Lone Operative Cannot be shot from more than 12 inches away unless it is part of an attached unit. No unit in this collection has it.
Damaged The bracket at which a big model starts hitting worse, usually -1 to hit for its last third of wounds.

Unit keywords

Infantry Foot models. They can move through and hide in terrain that stops others, and are what Blast and screens are for.
Mounted Cavalry: Bloodcrushers, Plague Drones, the Skullmaster, the Rendmaster and the Fateskimmer. Faster, tougher, and without the Infantry terrain rules.
Beast Fast animals: Flesh Hounds, Screamers, Beasts of Nurgle. They move through terrain like Infantry.
Swarm The Nurglings: bases of many small creatures counted as one model each.
Monster The big models: Be'lakor, the Greater Daemons, the Daemon Prince. They can shoot while in engagement range, cannot hide behind most terrain, and are what Anti-Monster weapons hunt.
Vehicle The War Dogs. The Shadow Legion admits no Vehicles, which is why they attend as Dreadblades instead.
Fly Can move over models and terrain as if they were not there, measuring the move across the top. Screamers' Slashing Dive and the monsters' mobility depend on it.
Character A hero model. Only Characters carry enhancements, and a Character with Leader can join a unit. Attached, it cannot be picked out except by Precision.
Epic Hero A named Character: one per army, no enhancements. The Shadow Legion bans every Epic Hero except Be'lakor, which is why Skulltaker and Karanak are out.
Battleline The army's core troops: up to six of one Battleline datasheet instead of three, and here the unit type that gets destroyed models back from Daemonic Manifestation. Pink and Blue Horrors, Bloodletters, Nurglings, Legionaries and Cultists.
Psyker A model that uses Psychic abilities, and the target Anti-Psyker weapons want. Be'lakor, the Lord of Change, the Great Unclean One, the Fateskimmer, the Master of Possession and the Horrors.
Daemon The keyword shared by every Legiones Daemonica model, and by the Possessed and Warp Talons. Legiones Daemonica is the faction keyword that the army rules and Warp Rifts key off.
Legiones Daemonica The Chaos Daemons faction keyword. The army rules, Warp Rifts and the Greater Daemons' auras apply to units with it; the marines do not have it.
Heretic Astartes The Chaos Space Marines faction keyword. In this detachment those units gain Dark Pacts and Deep Strike, and together they may make up at most half the army.
Grenades The unit may use the Grenades stratagem: a nearby enemy unit takes mortal wounds on dice rolls of 4+. The marines have it; the daemons do not.
Warlord The model that leads the army; some missions and abilities key off it. Here it must be Be'lakor whenever he is in the list.

Glossary III · the game, and this army's words

Phases and dice

battle round One player turn each. Games run five battle rounds. Reinforcements may arrive from the second.
Command phase The start of your turn: gain 1 CP, take Battle-shock tests for units below half strength, and resolve abilities that trigger here. Primary objectives are scored at the start of it from the second round.
Battle-shock A test taken in the Command phase by units below half strength, and whenever a rule forces one: roll 2D6 and pass on your Leadership or more. A failed unit is Battle-shocked until your next Command phase: OC 0, no stratagems on it, and Desperate Escape tests if it Falls Back. This army makes the enemy take many of these at -1, and its own daemons pass at +1 and heal.
below half strength A unit with fewer than half its starting models, or a single model with fewer than half its wounds. Such units test Battle-shock each Command phase; Be'lakor's Pall of Despair forces tests on enemy units that are merely below Starting Strength.
Normal move Moving up to the Move characteristic. The unit may still shoot and charge afterwards.
Advance Move plus D6 inches, at the cost of shooting and charging that turn unless a rule allows it. Assault weapons may still fire; Khorne daemons here may still charge (Murderer's Cowl); Warp Surge lets a daemon in the shadow charge too.
Fall Back Moving out of engagement range. The unit cannot shoot or charge afterwards unless a rule allows it, and Battle-shocked units must take Desperate Escape tests to do it. Flamers may shoot after Falling Back.
Engagement Range Within 1 inch of an enemy model. Units in it fight in the Fight phase and can only shoot Pistols.
Charge Declare targets, roll 2D6, and move that far to get into engagement range with every target, or the charge fails and the unit stays where it was. A unit arriving 9 inches from the enemy needs a 9 on 2D6, which succeeds about a quarter of the time; from 6 inches it needs a 6, which succeeds more often than not.
Fight phase Units that charged fight first, then the players alternate. Each unit piles in up to 3 inches, makes its attacks, and consolidates up to 3 inches (6 with a Bloodmaster).
Consolidate The 3-inch move after fighting, towards the nearest enemy or an objective marker. A Bloodmaster's unit moves 6.
attack sequence Roll to hit against BS or WS, roll to wound with Strength against Toughness, the defender rolls the better of its modified armour save and its invulnerable save, then Damage comes off Wounds. Mortal wounds skip the whole thing.
wound roll Strength against Toughness: double or more wounds on 2+, higher on 3+, equal on 4+, lower on 5+, half or less on 6+. A Bloodletter's S5 hellblade wounds a T4 marine on 3+, a T5 marine on 4+, a T12 Knight on 6+.
mortal wounds Damage that skips the hit roll, wound roll and every save. Each mortal wound removes one wound, and they spill over to the next model. Only Feel No Pain stops them. This army deals them through Battle-shock failures, Devastating Wounds, Brass Stampede, Slashing Dive and Relentless Carnage.
Reserves Units held off the table. They arrive from the second battle round: Strategic Reserves at a table edge, Deep Strike units anywhere 9 inches from the enemy. The mission pack caps how much of the army may start in Reserves; check it before writing the whole army off the board.
Rapid Ingress The core stratagem (1 CP) that lets a unit in Reserves arrive at the end of the opponent's Movement phase instead of your own. The return half of the yo-yo; free for a Chaos Lord's own squad once per round.
Overwatch The core stratagem (1 CP) that lets a unit shoot at an enemy unit that moves or charges within 24 inches of it, in the enemy's turn. It hits only on unmodified 6s, so Torrent weapons, which hit automatically, are the ones that make it hurt.
Heroic Intervention The core stratagem that lets one of your units charge an enemy unit that just charged near it, in the enemy's Charge phase.
Desperate Escape When a Battle-shocked unit Falls Back, or a unit leaves the Bloodletters' Bane of Cowards, roll a D6 per model: on a 1 or 2 that model is destroyed.
command points The currency for stratagems: one gained each Command phase. Per the July briefing, a unit benefits from only one stratagem per phase this edition.
stratagem A one-off rule bought with CP in the moment. Core stratagems are shared by every army (Rapid Ingress, Overwatch, Grenades, Command Re-roll); detachment stratagems are the Shadow Legion's own.
enhancement An upgrade bought with points for one Character that is not an Epic Hero: each enhancement once per army, at most three in total. Fade to Darkness, Leaping Shadows, Mantle of Gloom and Malice Made Manifest are the Shadow Legion's.
detachment The army's rules package: a rule, enhancements and stratagems. This edition prices detachments in Detachment Points so two can be combined; the tournament lists pair Shadow Legion (2 DP) with Cavalcade of Chaos (1 DP).
line of sight A unit can be targeted if any part of one of its models can be seen from the attacker. The Rendmaster's Blood Throne and most shooting need it.
cover What terrain gives a target. Per the July briefing, in this edition it makes the target harder to hit rather than improving its save, and quiet infantry inside terrain cannot be seen from beyond 15 inches. Read the core rules for the exact wording.

Missions and scoring

objective marker The points on the table you score for controlling, contested within 3 inches by Objective Control. Killing the enemy is only ever a means to standing on more of these.
primary mission The primary is the mission's fixed scoring, usually holding markers at the start of your Command phase. Secondaries are the extra goals drawn or chosen each game: actions, kills, positions. Both matter; most games are lost on the secondaries.
action A task a unit performs instead of shooting or charging to score a secondary. The Legionaries' second job.
Purge the Foe The disposition the Shadow Legion's missions use, as the July briefing described it: extra points each round for destroying more enemy units than you lose. Cheap screens that die for nothing feed it to the opponent.
deployment zone Your third of the table at the start; the opponent's is theirs. The Shadow of Chaos begins in yours and spreads to No Man's Land and then to theirs as you take their markers.
No Man's Land The middle of the table between the deployment zones, where most of the objectives sit. Hold half of its markers at the start of a phase and it is inside your shadow for that phase.
Strike Force The 2,000-point game size, the one every list in this repo is built to.
points Every unit's cost, set by the Munitorum Field Manual and updated a few times a year. Every number in this repo carries a snapshot date because of that.
WYSIWYG What you see is what you get: the models must carry the weapons the list says. Tournaments enforce it; kitchen tables do not.

This army's words

Shadow of Chaos The army rule's zone: your deployment zone always, No Man's Land and the enemy zone when you hold half their markers, and 6 inches around Be'lakor and each Greater Daemon. Inside it your daemons pass Battle-shock at +1 and heal or regrow; enemies test at -1 and bleed mortal wounds.

brick A full ten-model unit with its Herald attached, here almost always a Bloodmaster and ten Bloodletters.
bomb A unit that deep-strikes to kill one thing and does not expect to survive the reply: the Possessed with the Master of Possession.

Daemonic Terror The other half: an enemy unit inside the shadow, or within 6 inches of a Greater Daemon, takes Battle-shock tests at -1 and suffers D3 mortal wounds when it fails.

How the army works

The Shadow of Chaos
Your deployment zone is always in shadow; No Man's Land joins it in any phase where you hold at least half of its objectives, and the enemy zone joins when you hold half of theirs. Daemonic Manifestation: a daemon unit in shadow adds 1 to its Battle-shock roll and, on a pass, heals D3 or, if Battleline, returns up to D3 destroyed non-Character models. Daemonic Terror: an enemy unit in shadow, or within 6" of a Greater Daemon, tests Battle-shock at -1 and takes D3 mortal wounds when it fails.
Warp Rifts
A daemon deep-striking wholly inside the shadow, or wholly within 6" of a friendly Greater Daemon of its own god, can be set up more than 6" from the enemy instead of 9".
Dark Pacts
When a unit with it shoots or fights it may take a Leadership test: fail and it suffers D3 mortal wounds, and either way its weapons gain Lethal Hits or Sustained Hits 1 for the phase. Here Be'lakor and every Heretic Astartes unit have it; Be'lakor passes automatically.

Role

Warlord, umbrella and shadow engine
