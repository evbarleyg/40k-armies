Open threads

  • Pick a list and buy for it. The scout priced the finish line: B (Festering Court) ≈ $39, F (Triple Monster) ≈ $109–188, A (Yo-Yo Court) ≈ $91–117 — B and F are the recommended starts. E and F both hinge on one Bloodthirster. Every listing in the shopping tables is weeks old: re-run the scout (tools/) before buying, read-only as always.
  • Inventory the box against the collection page: the photo-extras (≈3 Beasts of Nurgle, Plague Drones, ≈10 Bloodletters, possible Terminators) decide whether B and D are as cheap as they look.
  • Points will move again. Everything is MFM v1.1 (2026-07-22); values marked ~ and the Dreadblades allowance want a check in the official app before an event.
  • Transport was asked about and never sized: N52 magnets in the bases plus a steel-lined box or rack for Be'lakor, the Lord of Change and the War Dogs; foam only for infantry; magnetise greater-daemon wings.
  • Market data is a July snapshot. Refresh the scorecard with scrape.js → data/raw_listings.psv → python3 build.py, then photo-sweep anything BUY-grade before believing it.
  • Publishing. This repo is public, so GitHub Pages is one setting away (repo Settings → Pages → main / root). daemon-quartermaster can be archived — everything in it now lives here — or opened to ebg-ant if it should stay in play.
