### B6.4 'Eavy Metal Be'lakor (2021) — best available paint reconstructions (official recipe NOT found verbatim)
- No Warhammer Community "how to paint Be'lakor" text article or Citadel Colour app recipe surfaced in search; the official video exists (B6.2) but its paint list could not be read. The following are the closest documented recipes:
- **Box-art emulation, RookieBrushes / Paint Pad (96 steps, 58 paints, May 2024)** — https://paintpad.app/recipes/3837-belakor-the-dark-master . Skin: "basecoats with Abaddon Black, drybrushed with Stegadon Scale Green, then more lightly with a 1:1 mix of Stegadon Scale Green and Sons of Horus Green, followed by selective highlights with a 1:1 mix of Sons of Horus Green and Karak Stone" (quoted from search summary) — i.e. the studio skin reads as a **black-to-dark-teal-grey**, not neutral grey. Sword flames/smoke: glazed "Xereus Purple", tips "Corvus Black", highlights "Temple Guard Blue and Baharroth Blue" for blue portions and "Xereus Purple mixed with Baharroth Blue" for purple portions. Bronze armour with verdigris: "Sotek Green and Kabalite Green … 1:1 … run into recesses, then … White Scar". Wing/leathery parts: "1:1 Rhinox Hide and XV-88 … XV-88 … Ushabti Bone". Loincloth: "Wazdakka Red & Screamer Pink" shaded "Khorne Red & Barak-Nar Burgundy". Gold: "Retributor Armour, Silver RLM01 Metallic and Dryad Bark" washed "Reikland Fleshshade and Agrax Earthshade". Eyes: "Averland Sunset … Yriel Yellow … Ice Yellow" (all quoted from search summaries).
- **Goonhammer "How to Paint Everything: Be'lakor" (Daniel "Skails" Rodenberg, 2021)** — https://www.goonhammer.com/how-to-paint-everthing-belakor/ (mirror https://www.tabletopbattles.com/how-to-paint-everthing-belakor/). Skin & wings: "base coat of Vallejo Model Color German Grey", "Pro Acryl Coal Black painted into the recesses", "Basalt Grey … 1:1 mix of Basalt Grey and Vallejo Game Color Stonewall Grey … Stonewall Grey … upper edge highlights, and Citadel Pallid Wych Flesh as a spot highlight on face and horns", then "Nuln Oil … liberally". Blade of Shadows: "Coal Black … on the bottom half of the sword. The bottom of the flames … Pro Acryl Dark Blue Grey. A mix of Dark Blue Grey and Pro Acryl Turquoise … into the recesses" (pale grey-turquoise spectral flame). Blood: "Vallejo Game Color Bloody Red … Citadel Wild Rider Red" (quoted from search summaries).
- **Cob Wargaming quick guide** (drybrush method close to the official video's approach): "heavy drybrush of Eshin Grey, being sure to leave plenty of black in the recesses… lightly drybrushing Administratum Grey … going quite heavily on the face"; blade "drybrushed with Stormhost Silver, picking out the edges, point and the central ridge" (quoted from search summaries) — https://cobwargaming.wixsite.com/cobwargaming/be-lakor-painting-guide
- Other high-end references: Richard Gray display tutorials (face/horns, abs; desaturated Pink Horror mixes) https://richardgraycreations.com/all-tutorials-belakor-the-dark-master/ ; Lil Legend Studio 2022 guide https://lillegendstudio.com/2022/04/11/how-to-paint-belakor-the-dark-master/ ; Duncan Rhodes video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKMrvQKqh8I ; airbrush skin (Transparent Blue → Sky Blue from below) https://spikeybits.com/warhammer-40k/how-to-airbrush-belakors-skin-next-level-painting-tutorial/ ; Adeptus Ars https://www.adeptusars.com/tutorials/how-to-paint-belakor-the-dark-master
- Consensus visual read of the studio model to carry into the vision doc: near-black skin lifted with cold blue/teal-greys; warm bronze/verdigris armour and gold; purple-brown membranes; the sword's flame is the single pale, cold light source (grey-white → turquoise → purple smoke); small warm accents (yellow eyes, red gore).

### B6.5 House Korvax (Be'lakor's Knight house) — official look
- Colours: "grey and white" (WarCom, above); heraldry: "simply a raven, its wings raised in a subtle mockery of House Raven, whose own badge does not display an avian" (quoted from search summary) — https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/House_Korvax ; https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/House_Korvax . ## B7. COMMUNITY SHADOW LEGION / LEGION OF THE FIRST PRINCE / DISCIPLES OF BE'LAKOR ARMIES

Caveat: search summaries rarely describe photos, and Reddit/Instagram/Facebook content was mostly unreachable, so several entries below are documented projects whose exact palette I could only partially confirm. I list 14; the strongest "one-army visual language" examples are 1, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10.

1. **TheChirurgeon (Robert Jones), Goonhammer "Road Through 2025" – Shadow Legion (40k)** — Part 12 "Testing the Shadow Legion" https://www.tabletopbattles.com/thechirurgeons-road-through-2025-part-12-testing-the-shadow-legion ; Part 13 https://www.goonhammer.com/thechirurgeons-road-through-2025-part-13-back-to-the-ettin-league/ ; tag https://www.goonhammer.com/tag/shadow-legion/ . Approach: existing daemon collection (long-standing, god-coloured Khorne core – Bloodcrushers "that came on 50mm bases decades ago" rebased to 90mm ovals, new Flesh Hounds) re-mustered around a newly painted Be'lakor; on Be'lakor's skin he notes the basecoat came out "a little more blue than I'd like… may want to do more of a purple/gray" (quoted from search summary). Reads as one army through consistent (re)basing and a cool purple-grey centrepiece rather than repainting the daemons.
2. **Mike Pestilens, Warphammer – "Legion of Shadow" Disciples of Be'lakor & 2025 Shadow Legion tournament armies (40k)** — https://warphammer40k.com/legion-of-shadow-disciples-of-belakor-tournament-report/ ; https://warphammer40k.com/disciples-of-shadow-the-complete-guide-to-playing-disciples-of-belakor/ ; https://warphammer40k.com/rocky-mountain-daemons-my-run-with-shadow-legion-at-the-rocky-mountain-open/ (5-1, 8th of 226). Competitive multi-god daemon collection under Be'lakor; painting approach NOT retrievable from summaries — include for list-shape/photo reference only.
3. **"Sam", Arc40k ArcArmy Challenge – Disciples of Be'lakor (40k, 2022–Mar 2023), posts #1–#8** — https://www.arc40k.com/post/disciples-of-be-lakor-8 ; https://www.arc40k.com/post/disciples-of-be-lakor-7 ; #2 https://www.arc40k.com/post/arcarmy-challenge-disciples-of-be-lakor-i-ve-got-my-ticket-have-you-got-yours . Approach: a normally "muted and grimdark with oils and weathering powders" painter deliberately built the whole force on "the colours of the warp – … magenta/purple/blue – highlighted with some flesh tones to mimic real skin but with a twist" (quoted from search summaries), i.e. every god's daemons pulled into one warp-magenta/purple/blue family; Flamers repainted three times and Bloodletters twice to get the balance right; unit identity carried by **custom-printed god-icon decals** on a green-stuff banner; presentation included an aged, "written in blood" narrative page. One army via a shared non-god palette + shared iconography. Same author's follow-on **House Korvax knights for Be'lakor** (Midnight Blue in place of black, raven-winged Knight conversion, "one War Dog for each Chaos God") — https://www.arc40k.com/post/things-are-moving — a direct precedent for tying War Dogs to a four-god daemon force.
4. **@theironwarsmith (Tumblr) – Disciples of Be'Lakor Chaos Space Marine warband (2021–22)** — https://www.tumblr.com/theironwarsmith/665101175281025025/the-first-champion-of-the-first-squad-of-the ; https://www.tumblr.com/theironwarsmith/669786287133343744/lord-asgul-teh%C3%A6ran-commander-of-the-disciples-of ; https://www.tumblr.com/theironwarsmith/665245036325306368/first-squad-heavy-gunner-former-space-wolves . Approach: renegades from many origins (ex-Space Wolves heavy gunner, ex-Thousand Sons Librarian, Lord Asgul Tehæran ex-"Emperor's Swords" captain) unified by "a somewhat muted palette" so that "bolder colours" stand out (quoted from search summary); 20 marines, 5 Chosen, 5 Terminators, 6 bikers. One army via desaturation of everything except chosen accents — the classic "shadow-touched mortals" recipe.
5. **No Rerolls blog – Legion of the First Prince (AoS, 2022→2025)** — planning https://norerolls.co.uk/2022/04/14/wishlisting-well-planning-projects-belakors-boys-and-ignoble-courts/ ; Completed Be'lakor https://norerolls.co.uk/2022/04/14/completed-belakor-the-dark-master/ ; 1k list https://norerolls.co.uk/2022/10/01/1k-aos-tourney-list-legion-of-the-first-prince/ ; Bloodletters https://norerolls.co.uk/2025/08/20/completed-20-bloodletters-of-khorne/ ; 4th-ed return https://norerolls.co.uk/2025/01/15/return-of-the-legion-belakor-and-his-pals-in-4e/ . Approach: the opposite pole — daemons in full classic god colours with an "old school bright presentation, with bright green static grass, red flowers (a hint of the bloodshed), and nice, bright green base rims" (quoted from search summary). One army purely via identical retro basing/rims. Useful counter-example: basing alone can unify four god palettes.
6. **Brookhammer – Be'lakor showcase (Dec 2022, with 360° video)** — https://www.brookhammer.com/2022/12/showcase-belakor-the-dark-master/ . ### B7-R. Recurring techniques observed / recommended across these sources
- **Cold near-black skin lifted by drybrush or glaze into blue-grey / teal-grey / purple-grey** as the "undivided/shadow" signifier (Brookhammer dark greys + purple; Bughaus Incubi Darkness→The Fang→Administratum Grey; Goonhammer German Grey→Basalt→Stonewall; RookieBrushes black→Stegadon Scale Green→Sons of Horus Green; TheChirurgeon aiming for "purple/gray") — URLs above.
- **Purple as the ethereal secondary** (wings, smoke, glazes) and **pale turquoise/blue-white as the "spectral flame" light colour** taken from the studio Blade of Shadows (RookieBrushes Temple Guard/Baharroth Blue + Xereus Purple; Goonhammer Dark Blue Grey + Turquoise) — https://paintpad.app/recipes/3837-belakor-the-dark-master ; https://www.goonhammer.com/how-to-paint-everthing-belakor/
- **One shared skin/base palette across all four gods, god identity pushed to details** — the standing forum advice for undivided daemons: "choose a common skin color and do details like horns, clothing, or armor in the appropriate god colors"; or "taking two colors … with each daemon type having varying degrees of both, and reversing the color scheme on some units; as long as your palette is the same you can mix things up and still have a unified look" (quoted from search summaries) — https://www.tapatalk.com/groups/wyrmlingx/unified-daemons-color-scheme-t11634.html ; https://www.dakkadakka.com/dakkaforum/posts/list/729892.page ; https://www.tga.community/forums/topic/18040-interesting-colour-schemes-for-chaos-daemons/ ; https://bolterandchainsword.com/topic/347329-daemon-alternate-paint-schemes/ . Arc40k's warp-magenta/purple/blue army and Terry Pike's blue-green army are worked examples.
- **A single light source as unifier (OSL)** — lava under-light (Max Soule), red glow on a monochrome body (Lil Legend), glowing Hellblades ("blackened but edged with glowing red embers" is the canonical Bloodletter blade description, quoted from search summary — https://warhammerfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Bloodletter); OSL how-tos https://www.tabletopbattles.com/how-to-paint-everything-object-source-lighting-osl/ , https://www.goonhammer.com/how-to-paint-everything-quick-and-easy-osl-the-20-min-methods
- **Same wash over everything + identical basing/rims** as the cheapest cohesion tools ("Consistent basing is the single most effective tool for army cohesion", quoted from search summary — https://warhammerguild.com/guides/warhammer-colour-scheme-ideas/); No Rerolls proves it even with full god colours.
- **Desaturate the mortals, not just the daemons** (theironwarsmith's muted CSM; House Korvax's black/grey/white knights; Arc40k's Midnight-Blue Korvax) so marines/knights sit "inside the shadow" and only sigils/lenses/weapon glows carry colour.
- **Shared iconography instead of shared colour** where colour must differ: custom god-icon decals and a Be'lakor banner (Arc40k), raven heraldry (Korvax).
- **Greyscale/"materialising" gradients** — black extremities fading up into colour or vice-versa (Legion Painting Studio's spawn-inspired blue-grey→black hands/feet; general greyscale method https://www.wargamingtradecraft.com/2019/09/multitone-black-and-white.html ; Malal black/white daemon tradition as precedent https://1d6chan.miraheze.org/wiki/Forces_of_Malal).

### B8.6 Possessed / Gal Vorbak — how painters bridge marine and daemon
