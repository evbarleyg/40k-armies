# Shadow Legion + Word Bearers thrall-host — Lore & Visual-Canon Research Digest

Prepared for: the "cohesive vision" document for Evan's second-hand Be'lakor / Shadow Legion Chaos Daemons army with a Word Bearers CSM contingent and two Chaos Knight War Dog Karnivores.
Date: 2026-08-11.

## READ THIS FIRST — method, limits, confidence labels

- **How this was gathered.** Direct page fetches (WebFetch/curl) are blocked by this environment's egress proxy for essentially every hobby/lore host (Lexicanum, Fandom, Warhammer Community, Goonhammer, YouTube, Reddit, Wahapedia, blogs, GitHub). Everything below therefore comes from **search-engine result summaries** of the cited pages: ~45 searches in this session plus two helper research sessions I ran in parallel with their own budgets (~145 searches on visual canon, ~160 on lore/naming). Their digests are merged here.
- **Quotes.** Any passage in quotation marks is wording that a search summary attributed to the cited URL — marked **(qvss)** = "quoted via search summary". Treat as very likely accurate but not eyeball-verified; re-check anything you will print verbatim.
- **Confidence labels:** a URL after a claim = sourced; `[uncited — general lore knowledge]` = well-established background I could not re-source (kept conservative, no invented specifics); `[UNVERIFIED]` = asked-for item I could not confirm either way. A consolidated gap list closes the report.
- **One correction to the brief's premise** (B6): the 2021 "Contrast+ How to Paint … Legion of the First Prince" videos are **not** Warhammer TV/GW output; they are Josh "Warhipster" Hill's personal channel. GW's genuinely official Be'lakor-force paint content is the April 2021 "How to Paint: Be'lakor" video, the 2022 "How to Paint: House Korvax Chaos Knights" video and the 'Eavy Metal studio model.

## THE ONE QUESTION THAT MATTERS MOST — is there a canonical Word Bearers ↔ Be'lakor link?

**Yes. It is campaign-book canon (War Zone Charadon, 2021) and it even comes with a named Word Bearers warband and Dark Apostle — but there is no novel-length treatment, so the *character* of the relationship is yours to write.**

What canon gives you, in order of strength:
1. **A named Word Bearers warband in Be'lakor's service.** In *War Zone Charadon Act I: The Book of Rust* / *Act II: The Book of Fire*, the Word Bearers **Dark Apostle Tsorr'Kanath** leads a warband called **"the Faithful"** (three companies and 27 armoured vehicles in the Book of Rust order of battle; relic: *Tsorr'Kanath's Forbidden Grimoire*, "bound in the hides of Daemons and wrapped in silver chains… unopened for centuries" (qvss)) — https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Tsorr'Kanath ; https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Faithful_(Warband) ; https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Tsorr'Kanath's_Forbidden_Grimoire ; review confirming the Book of Rust orders of battle https://spruesandbrews.com/2021/03/20/warhammer-40000-war-zone-charadon-act-1-the-book-of-rust-written-and-video-review/ . Typhus tasked the Faithful and the Alpha Legion **"Subtle Blades"** (Sorcerer **Yharron Thayl**) with pinning House Raven; "they ignored the Traveller's orders and instead began their own agenda, combining their Warbands together into the Disciples and creating or activating Chaos Cults across the Lirac Sub-sector" (qvss) — https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Disciples_(Warband) ; https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Charadon_Campaign . On the mining world **Dhaku**, Tsorr'Kanath summoned the Keeper of Secrets **Listho'shy'a the Sinuous** and the Disciples seized the Genestealer Patriarch Foresire Glutt "for their own nefarious purposes" — the opening moves of the ritual chain that "completed a summoning ritual and called forth Be'lakor into real space" (qvss) — https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Listho'shy'a ; https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Foresire_Glutt ; http://admechknight.blogspot.com/2021/07/warzone-charadon-book-of-fire-spoilers.html .
2. **The Word Bearers are named among Be'lakor's standing allies afterwards.** Codex: Chaos Knights (9th) on House Korvax: "Sometimes the Knights of House Korvax fight alongside the Alpha Legion or Word Bearers, themselves believed to be devotees of Be'lakor" (qvss) — https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/House_Korvax ; and after Kolossi became Korvosi, "Warriors in service to Be'lakor—Traitoris Militarum regiments and Heretic Astartes warbands alike—now congregate around Korvosi" (qvss) — https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/House_Korvax . Lexicanum/Fandom summaries of Be'lakor's own entry: "allying with the Word Bearers Traitor Legion, Be'lakor began his own assault upon the Charadon Sector" (qvss) — https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Be'lakor ; https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Be%27lakor .
3. **The rules canonise the pairing.** The 2021 *Disciples of Be'lakor* Army of Renown fields CSM + four-god daemons under him (https://www.warhammer-community.com/2021/07/09/have-your-cake-and-eat-it-with-belakors-disciples-the-dark-masters-mortal-and-daemonic-legion/), and the current **Shadow Legion** detachment (Index: Chaos Daemons, March 2025; retained in the 11th-ed. faction pack) is "a force of Daemons and Heretic Astartes in thrall to the Dark Master" (qvss) — https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/jl3uxdbu/index-chaos-daemons-updated-datasheets-and-a-new-detachment/ .

What canon does **not** give you: no Black Library novel, short story or audio pairs Be'lakor with the Word Bearers or Lorgar (searched Horus Heresy/Siege of Terra, Dawn of Fire, the Anthony Reynolds Word Bearers trilogy, White Dwarf; Be'lakor's principal 40k prose appearance is John French's Thousand Sons audio *Ahriman: The First Prince* — https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Ahriman:_The_First_Prince_(Audio_Drama)); the only "30k Word Bearers + Be'lakor" material is fan theming (https://bolterandchainsword.com/topic/300416-30k-word-bearers-belakor/). So the *why* and *how* of a Word Bearers Host in thrall to Be'lakor is a framing exercise — but one canon actively invites (see A3 for three framings; the strongest is simply "Evan's Host is, or splintered from, the Faithful").

# A. LORE

## A1. Be'lakor, the Dark Master

**Identity and titles.** "Be'lakor, the Dark Master, also known as the First Prince of Chaos and the One Who Heralds the Conquerors, is a Daemon Prince of Chaos Undivided; he is beholden to each of the Chaos Gods equally" (qvss) — https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Be'lakor . Also "the First-Damned" (https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Be%27lakor); the Warhammer Fantasy/End Times roll-call adds "the Harbinger, the Lord of Torment, the Messenger of the Great Undivided, the One Who Heralds the Conquerors, the Foretoken and the Bearer" plus "the Shadowlord" (his mortal guise on Albion) (qvss) — https://warhammerfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Be'lakor ; https://whfb.lexicanum.com/wiki/Be'lakor . Rules-text epithets you can reuse in prose: **Shadow Lord** (Warlord trait / 10th-ed. aura), **Lord of Torment** and **Shadow Form** (abilities), and Warhammer Community's informal "Shadow King" / "shadowy demigod" (https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/vBWk2WFM/earn-the-favour-of-the-dark-gods-to-make-your-massive-killing-machines-even-more-unstoppable/). His weapon, the **Blade of Shadows**, is "a unique etherblade… its ghostly form in eternal transience between shape and shadow… its essence changing in an instant from formless shadow to murderous edge at its master's whim" (qvss) — https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Be%27lakor .

**Origin — favoured by all four, then diminished.** "Be'lakor became the first mortal to be blessed by all four Ruinous Powers and became the first Daemon Prince. Granted his dark apotheosis by the combined will of the Chaos Gods as Chaos Undivided, he was given a portion of each of their power"; "For a time, the gods fought over Be'lakor as children might squabble over a favoured toy. However, they soon realised the folly of combining their might into a single vessel, as Be'lakor proved nearly uncontrollable"; "They soon began to raise up new Daemon Princes, each god choosing only Chaos Champions that would be loyal to them, and them alone. Be'lakor remained the strongest of the Daemon Princes, though his might was diminished as the gods spread their power among their other servants" (all qvss) — https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Be%27lakor ; https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Be'lakor . Antiquity: "Legends tell of Be'lakor ruling over mortal empires since the dawn of time, the Daemon Prince conquering a world and subjugating its people, forcing them to worship him as a god. When the race would fall into decline, ruined by Be'lakor's greed and malevolence, the Daemon would move on, finding a new burgeoning race to be his playthings" (qvss) — https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Be'lakor . Codex-review gloss: "his origins are shrouded in mystery… worshipped by various cultures for millennia… eternally jealous of playing second fiddle to the chaos gods' favored champions" — http://www.mengelminiatures.com/2022/09/review-codex-chaos-daemons.html .

**Character.** "Be'lakor remains a master of shadows, moving behind the veil of history to exert the will of the Chaos Gods upon the universe"; "Only when the moment is right does Be'lakor manifest his true and terrible form, striking down his enemies with the Blade of Shadows even as he rakes claws of psychic energy through the meat of their minds" (qvss) — https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Be'lakor ; https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Be%27lakor . "Like a petulant firstborn son, Be'lakor has always had a bitter jealousy toward anything or anyone that wins the favour of the Dark Gods" (qvss, Fandom). GW's product copy: "Wreathed in ethereal shadows and lifted high above the battlefield on monstrous wings, Be'lakor regards the efforts of his foes with a bitter scorn as they struggle in vain against his diabolical plans" (qvss) — https://www.warhammer.com/en-US/shop/Chaos-Daemons-Belakor-The-Dark-Master-2021 . AoS warscroll: "A winged nightmare wreathed in darkness and redolent with terrible powers… With shadow magic and daemonic might he obliterates his foes"; "Many puppets dance on Be'lakor's infernal strings. Few realise as much until their fate is sealed" (qvss) — https://wahapedia.ru/aos4/factions/slaves-to-darkness/Be-lakor-the-Dark-Master .

**The "curse": a player who is also a pawn.** In 40k the curse is framed as perpetual manipulation rather than a single hex: "When the Daemon Prince brought down a Champion of Nurgle, invariably he was doing Tzeentch's bidding, and while laying a warrior of the Blood God low he was fulfilling the will of Slaanesh"; "Be'lakor remains blind to these manipulations… his own thirst for power and the pleasure of proving his mastery over rival Champions of Chaos seeming enough to make him forget the sorcerous tethers the Dark Gods continually try to wind tightly around his neck" (qvss) — https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Be%27lakor . In the Fantasy/AoS lineage the humiliation is explicit: he bears "the shame of crowning new champions of Chaos (the current one being Archaon) but never holding that title himself" — https://spruesandbrews.com/2021/04/17/broken-realms-belakor-review/ ; his AoS cult's Dark Creed "holds the belief that Be'lakor – not Archaon – is the rightful Everchosen champion of Chaos" (qvss) — https://ageofsigmar.lexicanum.com/wiki/Chaos_Legionnaires . `[uncited — general lore knowledge]` In WHFB it was Tzeentch specifically who stripped him of form and made him the Harbinger who crowns each Everchosen; 40k keeps only the echo in the title "the One Who Heralds the Conquerors".

**Relationship with each god** (Warhammer Community, "Which Chaos God Does Be'lakor Really Serve?", 17 Nov 2021 — https://www.warhammer-community.com/2021/11/17/which-chaos-god-does-belakor-really-serve/ , all qvss): *Khorne* — "No matter his shadowy intentions, he finds himself on the battlefield often enough to please Khorne greatly." *Tzeentch* — "He's so meddlesome that Tzeentch has chained him up more than a handful of times. Each time the Lord of Torment has escaped." *Nurgle* — "Nurgle respects this kind of near-infinite longevity, and Be'lakor's love of cycles – scheme, win, get put in a box by a Chaos god, break out, rinse, and repeat." *Slaanesh* — "Everything Be'lakor does, he does it for his insatiable ego." *Verdict* — "Be'lakor is too similar to all four gods to serve any of them, which is his biggest strength and weakness. They see themselves in him and vice versa, causing them to lash out in petulant rage at their perceived failings."

**What official text says the Shadow Legion LOOKS like (the descriptive passages).** Two findings, both important for the vision document:
1. **There is no official statement that Be'lakor's daemons are physically recoloured/monochrome**, and no Warhammer Community article was found showing four-god daemons in a unified dark livery — in official photography Be'lakor stands beside daemons in standard god colours (finding by absence across both research passes; `[UNVERIFIED]` only in the sense that a negative can't be proven).
2. **What IS official is a rich, consistent vocabulary of environmental shadow that clings to everything in his service** — which is precisely the licence for a "painted in his shadow" army. The passages (all qvss):
   - Be'lakor: "Wreathed in ethereal shadows" (https://www.warhammer.com/en-US/shop/Chaos-Daemons-Belakor-The-Dark-Master-2021); "a nexus for occluding shadow, veiling his enemies' sight and leaving them helpless to parry the vicious blows of his servants both mortal and daemonic" (Book of Fire AoR text via https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k9ed/factions/chaos-daemons/Be-lakor); WHFB: "a creature of living shadows, tangible only when he wished to be, a master of terror and illusion who feasted upon mortal fears" (https://warhammerfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Be'lakor); AoS: "can shift his physical form to be as insubstantial as the mists of Ulgu" (https://wahapedia.ru/aos4/factions/slaves-to-darkness/Be-lakor-the-Dark-Master).
   - His servants: "Be'lakor's servants manifest echoes of his own supernatural powers, exuding terrifying auras or flickering in and out of reality amidst cowls of animate shadow" (Book of Fire Warlord-trait intro via the same Wahapedia page — probable, exact phrase not re-confirmed); "the pall of unnatural shadow that accompanies Be'lakor's Disciples" (https://www.warhammer-community.com/2021/07/09/have-your-cake-and-eat-it-with-belakors-disciples-the-dark-masters-mortal-and-daemonic-legion/).
   - Shadow Legion detachment (2025→2026): "Bound by coils of umbral energy, mortal and daemonic followers are swept up in Be'lakor's cloak of shadows as pawns returned to their master's hand"; "Be'lakor's aura falls across the field of battle, bearing servants both mortal and daemonic to war. Attuned to the gloom, these new arrivals combine their fire…"; "Even death does not free one from Be'lakor's grasp. Those who disappoint him may be dragged back through the veil, granted one last chance to impress their master or face the full extent of his wrath"; per god — Khorne: "The most murderous seek not to hide in the Dark Master's pall. The shadows extend before them, offering a rapid route to slaughter"; Tzeentch: "Mutative agents of change, touched by the Dark Master's umbral aura, summon illusory magicks…"; Nurgle: "Borne upon the gloaming wisp of Be'lakor's shadow, this debilitating curse atrophies muscle and withers flesh"; and the stratagem Fade to Darkness names them "Be'lakor's thralls" — https://www.tabletopbattles.com/detachment-focus-shadow-legion/ ; https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k10ed/factions/chaos-daemons/ ; https://www.40k.app/factions/chaos-daemons/detachments/shadow-legion .
   - House Korvax / Korvosi: "Where they appear the stars vanish from the heavens, blotted out by a heavy umbral pall. Light sources flicker and die, and supernatural dread creeps into the minds of those they face… The Knights themselves then strike from the shadows"; the Knights are "nightmarishly deformed"; "House Korvax are servants of shadow and terror; wherever they tread the skies darken and fear and madness spread"; WarCom: they "loom out of the cloying shadow that surrounds them like monolithic spectres"; Korvosi is "a Fallen Knight World of mist and shadow… enshrouded by a darkness so vast that it stretches into the surrounding void of space", registering "more as an absence against a backdrop of stars than a visible presence", with "hypnotic balefires", "immense chasms" and "gigantic land masses the size of continents that float in the skies, tethered by immense chains with links as broad as battleships" — https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/House_Korvax ; https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k9ed/factions/chaos-knights/ ; https://www.warhammer-community.com/2022/03/31/these-chaos-knight-dread-households-are-coming-to-stomp-on-the-galaxy/ ; https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Korvosi . A Knight who worships "a particular shadowy demigod can receive a tenebrous cowl in return… this umbral boon" makes it "gloom-touched" (WarCom on the Blessing of the Dark Master favour — https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/vBWk2WFM/earn-the-favour-of-the-dark-gods-to-make-your-massive-killing-machines-even-more-unstoppable/).
   - So the defensible narrative line is: **the Legion marches inside Be'lakor's shadow — an umbral pall, cowls of animate shadow, mist, dying light, coronas of darkness — while each daemon remains what its god made it.** The desaturated/monochrome hobby treatment (B6–B7) is a legitimate visualisation of that text, not a contradiction of it; so is "full god colours under one shared shadow/basing/light effect".

## A2. Why daemons of all four gods serve together under him — and how mortals fit

**In-lore justifications (all sourced).**
1. *He carries a portion of each god's power* and is "beholden to each of the Chaos Gods equally" — https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Be%27lakor ; https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Be'lakor . The Shadow Legion intro restates it: "Elevated to the status of Daemon Prince by the combined might of the four Dark Gods of Chaos, Be'lakor exudes unimaginable power" (qvss) — https://www.40k.app/factions/chaos-daemons/detachments/shadow-legion .
2. *Mixed motives — lured, coerced, bound.* "The daemons who serve Be'lakor do not necessarily do so willingly. While some have been lured with the promise of power, many are coerced or outright forcibly bound to his service"; "Over the centuries, Be'lakor has even learned the true names of multiple Greater Daemons, and several of these powerful creatures act as his reluctant generals" (qvss, AoS Legion of the First Prince) — https://ageofsigmar.lexicanum.com/wiki/Legion_of_the_First_Prince ; worked example: **Kazarkos**, a Bloodthirster "bound to Be'lakor against his will" through his true name — https://ageofsigmar.lexicanum.com/wiki/Kazarkos ; GW's one-liner: "some coerced, some enslaved, and some that find common cause in the destruction of empires" — https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/nKM194TU/find-out-which-armies-are-getting-upgrades-in-broken-realms-belakor/ . 40k's general true-name rule backs the mechanism: "Repeating a Daemon's True Name to it binds it to the speaker… To have a Daemon's name is to hold ultimate power over it" (qvss) — https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/True_name .
3. *The gods tolerate it because it serves the Great Game* (his victories always profit one god or another; they keep "sorcerous tethers" on him) — https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Be%27lakor .
4. *GW enforces the "balance of four" in rules*: the AoR's one-of-each-god requirement (https://www.warhammer-community.com/2021/07/09/have-your-cake-and-eat-it-with-belakors-disciples-the-dark-masters-mortal-and-daemonic-legion/) and Shadow Legion's per-god boons "and additional abilities for mortal followers, reflecting their submission to Be'lakor's dark will" (qvss) — https://www.belloflostsouls.net/2025/03/warhammer-40k-chaos-daemons-index-gets-a-new-update.html .

**How mortal followers are described, rewarded and punished.** Official nouns: "Heretic Astartes in thrall to the Dark Master", "mortal follower" (https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/jl3uxdbu/index-chaos-daemons-updated-datasheets-and-a-new-detachment/); "Be'lakor's thralls", "pawns returned to their master's hand", failures "dragged back through the veil, granted one last chance to impress their master" (https://www.tabletopbattles.com/detachment-focus-shadow-legion/); "his mortal disciples" (Book of Fire blurb); Korvax nobles "in thrall to a fell Daemon Prince of Chaos", their Knights "suffused with the power of the Warp" and "remade to Be'lakor's design" (https://1d6chan.miraheze.org/wiki/Warhammer_40,000/9th_Edition_Tactics/Chaos_Knights ; https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Arvael). The *reward* is a share of his shadow: servants "manifest echoes of his own supernatural powers… cowls of animate shadow" (Book of Fire, via https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k9ed/factions/chaos-daemons/Be-lakor); Knights receive "a tenebrous cowl" (WarCom, URL above); AoS Legionnaires' "former allegiances [are] cast aside upon swearing loyalty to the Dark Master", they abandon their names, and Mutandors "embrace the gifts of their dark master and combine mortal skill with daemonic power" (qvss) — https://warcrier.net/docs/warbands/chaos/chaos-legionnaires/background-tables ; https://ageofminiatures.com/chaos-legionnaires-warcry-warband/ .

**"Shadow-marked" and marks/icons — verdict.** Exact-phrase searches for "shadow-marked"/"shadow marked" with Be'lakor returned nothing; it is **not** official terminology (safe to use as your own coinage). Nearest official phrasings: "in thrall to the Dark Master", "Be'lakor's thralls", "mortal followers", "Disciples of Shadow", "servants of shadow and terror", "gloom-touched" (URLs above). No official Be'lakor sigil for Disciples was found (a Bolter & Chainsword thread asking exactly this reaches no canonical answer — https://bolterandchainsword.com/topic/371458-sigil-of-the-dark-master-belakors-mark/); the model itself carries an eight-pointed-star scar on the torso (https://www.belloflostsouls.net/2026/07/warhammer-40k-belakor-the-first-daemon-prince.html) and the AoS Legion uses generic Undivided "Eightfold Doom-Sigil" iconography (https://ageofsigmar.lexicanum.com/wiki/Legion_of_the_First_Prince). Book of Fire leaves Word Bearers/Alpha Legion Disciples in their own Legion identity — they only swap their Legion *trait* (https://www.warhammer-community.com/2021/07/09/have-your-cake-and-eat-it-with-belakors-disciples-the-dark-masters-mortal-and-daemonic-legion/).

**His habit of subverting other powers' followers** (the pattern that makes a "thrall-host" plausible): House Raven → House Korvax via suborned Sacristans (https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/House_Korvax); the Faithful and the Subtle Blades peeled off Typhus's campaign (https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Disciples_(Warband)); Iron Warriors Warsmith Shon'tu, "united in their desire to see Abaddon's Black Crusade upstaged" (qvss, https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Be%27lakor); a bargain with Ahriman for Ctesias's soul (https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Ahriman:_The_First_Prince_(Audio_Drama)); a seat in Abaddon's inner circle; any Chaos Knight house can take the Blessing of the Dark Master by worshipping him (https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/vBWk2WFM/earn-the-favour-of-the-dark-gods-to-make-your-massive-killing-machines-even-more-unstoppable/); in AoS, Eternus "has succeeded in turning almost the entire Eighth Circle of the Varanguard to his cause", plus "spies and turncoats at the courts of Hagg Nar and Druchiroth" (https://ageofsigmar.lexicanum.com/wiki/Be'lakor ; https://ageofsigmar.lexicanum.com/wiki/Eternus).

**Framing the Word Bearers ↔ Be'lakor relationship (three options, descending canon support).**
1. **"We are the Faithful" (fully canonical).** Evan's Host *is* (or is a splinter/successor of) Tsorr'Kanath's Faithful: the Word Bearers who abandoned Typhus's war, merged with the Subtle Blades as "the Disciples", raised cults across the Lirac sub-sector, summoned Listho'shy'a on Dhaku, and finally called Be'lakor into realspace; now they muster from mist-drowned Korvosi beside House Korvax (all URLs in the key-question box). Nothing needs inventing except squad-level names — and Codex: Chaos Knights already calls such Word Bearers "devotees of Be'lakor" (https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/House_Korvax).
2. **"From the Word to the First Prince" (theological, canon-consistent).** Word Bearers venerate Chaos Undivided and treat daemons as holy and possession as sacrament (WarCom 2019; Gal Vorbak "Blessed Sons"); Be'lakor is Undivided apotheosis in a single being ("by the combined might of the four Dark Gods"). A Dark Apostle deciding the First Prince is the Word made flesh — heresy within heresy — fits a Legion riven by Erebus/Kor Phaeron factionalism and Host-level power struggles (34th Host). The rules even dramatise it: Disciples CSM *replace their Legion trait* with Disciples of Shadow (https://www.goonhammer.com/war-zone-charadon-the-book-of-fire-review-chaos/).
3. **"Subverted, like House Raven" (Be'lakor's M.O.).** He whispers to artisans until Thrones turn (Kolossi), binds greater daemons by true name (Kazarkos), takes turncoats' names away (Legionnaires) and drags failures "back through the veil". A Master of Possession who bound one shadow too many and found the binding ran the other way makes "thrall-host" literal.
Recommendation: run 1 as the spine, 2 as the Host's creed, 3 as the doubt its Coryphaus never voices.

**Recurring techniques (observed across the above; sources as cited there).**
- *Cold near-black skin lifted into blue-grey / teal-grey / purple-grey* as the undivided/shadow signifier (Brookhammer; Bughaus Incubi Darkness→The Fang→Administratum Grey; Goonhammer German Grey→Basalt→Stonewall; RookieBrushes Abaddon Black→Stegadon Scale Green→Sons of Horus Green; TheChirurgeon aiming for "purple/gray").
- *Purple as the ethereal secondary* (wings, smoke, glazes) and *pale turquoise/blue-white as the spectral-flame light colour*, both lifted from the studio Blade of Shadows (https://paintpad.app/recipes/3837-belakor-the-dark-master ; https://www.goonhammer.com/how-to-paint-everthing-belakor/).
- *One shared skin/base palette across all four gods, identity pushed to details* — standing forum advice: "choose a common skin color and do details like horns, clothing, or armor in the appropriate god colors", or two shared colours in varying ratios per unit (https://www.tapatalk.com/groups/wyrmlingx/unified-daemons-color-scheme-t11634.html ; https://www.dakkadakka.com/dakkaforum/posts/list/729892.page ; https://www.tga.community/forums/topic/18040-interesting-colour-schemes-for-chaos-daemons/ ; https://bolterandchainsword.com/topic/347329-daemon-alternate-paint-schemes/); Arc40k and Terry Pike are worked examples.
- *A single light source as unifier (OSL)*: lava under-light (Soule), red glow on monochrome (Lil Legend), ember-edged hellblades; how-tos https://www.tabletopbattles.com/how-to-paint-everything-object-source-lighting-osl/ ; https://www.goonhammer.com/how-to-paint-everything-quick-and-easy-osl-the-20-min-methods .
- *Same wash over everything + identical basing/rims* — "Consistent basing is the single most effective tool for army cohesion" (https://warhammerguild.com/guides/warhammer-colour-scheme-ideas/); No Rerolls proves it even with full god colours.
- *Desaturate the mortals too* (theironwarsmith's muted CSM; Korvax's near-black/grey/white knights; Arc40k's Midnight-Blue Korvax) so marines and Knights sit inside the shadow and only sigils/lenses/weapon-glows carry colour.
- *Shared iconography where colour must differ* (custom god-icon decals + Be'lakor banner; raven heraldry).
- *"Materialising" gradients* — colour fading to black at extremities or the reverse (Legion Painting Studio's spawn-inspired blue-grey→black hands/feet; greyscale method https://www.wargamingtradecraft.com/2019/09/multitone-black-and-white.html).
- *Basing as the shadow*: dark ash/slate, drifting mist (cotton/water effects), painted shadow tendrils — echoing Korvosi's "mist and shadow" and the Cursed Skies.

**B8.6 Possessed / Gal Vorbak — bridging marine and daemon (documented techniques).**
- Flesh erupting from red plate with trauma at the seam: Vallejo Pale Flesh over the red, then "Blood for the Blood God over parts of the flesh, in particular where it meets armour plating or any part of the flesh where things protrude" (qvss, Goonhammer HTPE); Rakarth Flesh for flesh, skin-tabards and "flesh cables" so organic and cloth read as one material (https://www.thebeardbunker.com/2020/05/bearers-of-word.html).
- Blur the boundary with shade: "Nuln Oil where the purple flesh meets armour, which darkens the skin-tone significantly and makes it harder to distinguish where armour ends and skin begins" (qvss) — http://www.bolterandchainsword.com/topic/297060-daemons-wip-musings-gal-vorbak/page-2 ; step-by-step possessed skin http://masterdarksol.blogspot.com/2013/07/step-by-step-possesseddaemon-skin.html ; Tale of Painters purple Possessed flesh https://taleofpainters.com/2023/04/tutorial-how-to-paint-purple-chaos-possessed-flesh/ .
- **Match the daemonflesh to the daemon allies, not to human skin**: Legion Painting Studio's blue-grey→black "ethereal" Gal Vorbak is the direct template — paint the emerging flesh in the Shadow Legion skin recipe (cold black-grey/purple-grey) so the Possessed read as Be'lakor's daemons wearing Word Bearers plate (https://legionpaintingstudio.co.uk/2023/12/10/warhammer-40000-gal-vorbak-word-bearers/).
- Muted red + purple recess (Leviathan Purple under Word Bearers Red) already pulls WB armour toward the daemons' purple shadow tone (https://apologentsia.blogspot.com/2019/10/inload-bearers-of-word.html). Sculpt references for fused flesh/armour and script: FW Gal Vorbak https://www.warhammer.com/en-WW/shop/Word-Bearers-Legion-Gal-Vorbak ; Mhara Gal https://www.warhammer.com/en-GB/shop/Word-Bearers-Mhara-Gal-Tainted-Dreadnought ; videos https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AWqa0NI-CrI , https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bQ1Bbg1masY .

**B8.7 Darker / Be'lakor-serving Word Bearers.** No fully documented "Word Bearers in black/red painted explicitly as Disciples of Be'lakor" army was found `[absent]`; nearest precedents are theironwarsmith's muted multi-origin Disciples CSM (B7 #4), the 3rd-ed "wine-coloured" studio scheme and dark Gal-Vorbak-Red Heresy recipes (B8.1), deeper shading with Carroburg Crimson / Nuln Oil Gloss (https://porchhammer.blogspot.com/2017/05/army-selection-and-paint-scheme-word.html), and Siege Studios' crimson Be'lakor for a Word Bearers client (B7). Since canon keeps Disciples in their own Legion colours (they only swap the trait) and House Korvax shows GW's idea of "Be'lakor's mortals" as a dark conventional livery plus *atmosphere* (mist, coronas of darkness), the well-supported direction is: keep the Word Bearers recognisably Word Bearers (crimson, script, Latros Sacrum) and signal thralldom by *additions* — a shadow-glyph or Be'lakor device where a Host badge would sit, greaves/lower armour glazed toward black ("standing in his pall"), the daemons' cold spectral accent on lenses/plasma/ritual flames, Possessed flesh in the daemon skin recipe, and the army-wide shadow/mist basing.
