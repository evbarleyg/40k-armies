## L1. What Be'lakor's daemons / Disciples LOOK like in official text (shadow vocabulary)

**Be'lakor himself**
- warhammer.com product page ("Be'lakor, the Dark Master"): "Wreathed in ethereal shadows and lifted high above the battlefield on monstrous wings, Be'lakor regards the efforts of his foes with a bitter scorn as they struggle in vain against his diabolical plans" (qvss); also calls him "a creature of nightmare" and "supernaturally skilled in both martial and magical arts" (qvss). https://www.warhammer.com/en-US/shop/Chaos-Daemons-Belakor-The-Dark-Master-2021
- Codex-derived text (9th ed. era, via Lexicanum/Fandom): "Only when the moment is right does Be'lakor manifest his true and terrible form, striking down his enemies with the Blade of Shadows even as he rakes claws of psychic energy through the meat of their minds" (qvss); "a master of shadows, moving behind the veil of history" (qvss). https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Be%27lakor ; https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Be'lakor
- Blade of Shadows: "a unique etherblade… its ghostly form in eternal transience between shape and shadow… its essence changing in an instant from formless shadow to murderous edge at its master's whim" (qvss). https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Be%27lakor
- 9th-ed. Army of Renown rules text (Book of Fire → Wahapedia): "Be'lakor is a nexus for occluding shadow, veiling his enemies' sight and leaving them helpless to parry the vicious blows of his servants both mortal and daemonic" (qvss). http://wahapedia.ru/wh40k9ed/factions/chaos-daemons/ and https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k9ed/factions/chaos-daemons/Be-lakor

**Stated goals / outlook, Era Indomitus**
- Core motive: "bitter jealousy towards anyone who wins the favour of the Dark Gods"; he "has begun shadowing… Abaddon", "spent centuries watching over him and influencing events… knowing on some level that their fates were bound"; "worked his way close to the Warmaster of Chaos, earning a place in Abaddon's inner circle"; "surely plotting against Abaddon… seeking to undermine the victory intended for the Warmaster of Chaos by the Dark Gods and claim the power and glory of conquest for himself" (qvss; Dataslate 2013/Codex text as summarised). https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Be%27lakor ; https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Dataslate:_Be'lakor,_the_Dark_Master_(Background_Book)
- Ancient pattern: "conquering a world and subjugating its people, forcing them to worship him as a god… When the race would fall into decline… the Daemon would move on" (qvss). https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Be'lakor

## L3. Why daemons of all four gods serve him; how mortals are described/rewarded; subversion examples

**Official justifications**
- Origin: "Granted his dark apotheosis by the combined will of the Chaos Gods as Chaos Undivided, he was given a portion of each of their power"; he is "beholden to each of the Chaos Gods equally"; the gods "realised the folly of combining their might into a single vessel, as Be'lakor proved nearly uncontrollable" (qvss). https://warhammer40k.fandom.com/wiki/Be%27lakor ; https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Be'lakor
- Coercion/true names (AoS Legion of the First Prince, directly transferable flavour): "The daemons who serve Be'lakor do not necessarily do so willingly. While some have been lured with the promise of power, many are coerced or outright forcibly bound to his service"; "Over the centuries, Be'lakor has even learned the true names of multiple Greater Daemons, and several of these powerful creatures act as his reluctant generals"; the legion is "a wild gathering of daemonic footsoldiers of Khorne, Tzeentch, Nurgle, and Slaanesh" that "has destroyed a countless number of empires and kingdoms over the aeons" (qvss). https://ageofsigmar.lexicanum.com/wiki/Legion_of_the_First_Prince ; https://wahapedia.ru/aos/factions/legion-of-the-first-prince/
- WarCom Broken Realms line (you have): "some coerced, some enslaved, and some that find common cause in the destruction of empires". https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/nKM194TU/find-out-which-armies-are-getting-upgrades-in-broken-realms-belakor/
- Balance-of-Four enforced in rules: Disciples of Be'lakor may not take a second unit of one god's daemons without one of each other god (WarCom 2021) https://www.warhammer-community.com/2021/07/09/have-your-cake-and-eat-it-with-belakors-disciples-the-dark-masters-mortal-and-daemonic-legion/ ; Shadow Legion gives each god's daemons a distinct boon "and additional abilities for mortal followers, reflecting their submission to Be'lakor's dark will" (qvss, BoLS paraphrase of WarCom). https://www.belloflostsouls.net/2025/03/warhammer-40k-chaos-daemons-index-gets-a-new-update.html
