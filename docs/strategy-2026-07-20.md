## The gameplan: "The Long Shadow"

Your army wants to win by **fear, position, and arrival timing** — not attrition.

**Deployment.** Nurglings infiltrate onto midfield objectives (they're your sticky scorers). Havocs on the home objective with lascannon sightlines. Horrors castle behind terrain. Be'lakor and the Lord of Change either lurk behind LOS-blockers or go into deep strike — the detachment's signature is that essentially everything can.

**Turns 1–2: weather or vanish.** Against shooting armies, put the whole army in reserve and give them two turns of shooting at Nurglings and terrain. Against melee armies, castle: Horrors screen, monsters counterpunch anything that touches the line. Be'lakor's shadow aura is your umbrella — keep fragile units inside it.

**Turns 2–3: the double arrival.** Land Be'lakor and the Lord of Change *together*, supported by the Possessed + Master of Possessions, at whichever flank is thinnest. Two flying monsters plus a melee bomb arriving simultaneously is more than most opponents can answer — they'll kill one threat and lose the game to the other two.

**Scoring engine.** Pink Horrors die into Blues, Blues into Brimstones — one purchase, three waves of objective-sitting bodies. Legionaries do actions/secondaries. War Dogs (if legal in your list) hunt vehicles and grab far objectives at speed. You out-body and out-position; you don't out-fight.

**Battleshock pressure.** The detachment and Be'lakor both lean on fear mechanics — pile them onto whoever's contesting an objective, not the strongest enemy unit. Flipping one objective at the right moment wins more games than a dead Terminator squad.

## What's missing (in priority order)

1. **A melee hammer that isn't a character.** Your only real punch is Possessed + monsters. 10 painted **Bloodletters** (~$40–70 on eBay, constant supply) — and note the detachment gives Khorne units advance-and-charge, making them genuinely scary.
2. **Speed for secondaries.** The [$180 Seekers lot](https://www.ebay.com/itm/297286491088) (offer ~$130): 10 Seekers + 3 Flamers + 3 more Nurgling bases, painted. Fills the fast-scorer gap exactly. Ignore its proxy Stalkers.
3. **Mobile CSM units you DON'T own** — Raptors / Warp Talons / Chosen. This is the real argument for the [$650 Night Lords lot](https://www.ebay.com/itm/188656011886): its Raptors + Warp Talons + jump Lord add the mobility your list lacks, its duplicated units become alternates, its Terminators/vehicles seed a future pure-CSM army, and its primer squads are your paint project — painted in YOUR red scheme, not their blue.
4. **Skip:** Red Corsairs (duplicates your MoP/Legionaries/Possessed almost exactly), any Epic Hero (banned), a third greater daemon (points don't fit), the $1,375 Khorne army (its centerpiece is banned).
