Shadow Legion — 11th Edition Research Digest (2026-07-26)

Deep-research verified (97-agent run, 3-vote adversarial verification). Full output archived in session task wnq11tznq.

Verified rules (11th ed, as of MFM v1.1 2026-07-22)

  • Shadow Legion persists in 11th: 2 Detachment Points, Purge the Foe disposition. Rated among the stronger Daemons detachments post-July-update (Daemons overall "Slight Loser"; Legion of Excess strongest, Scintillating Legion hardest hit).
  • First Prince of Chaos (detachment rule), current wording:
  • Khorne: shoot and charge after Advancing
  • Tzeentch: Stealth + −1 to be hit in melee (changed from generic −1 to hit, to stop stacking with new cover)
  • Nurgle: −1 to wound when attack S > unit T
  • Slaanesh: immune to ALL snap shooting (expanded from Overwatch-only)
  • Heretic Astartes: Dark Pacts + Deep Strike (Be'lakor auto-passes his pact tests)
  • Master of Possession is LEGAL (Wahapedia + 40k.app both list it; the Tabletop Battles commentary contradicting this is stale). Allowed HA units: Chaos Lord ×3 variants, Sorcerer ×2, MoP, Terminators, Chosen, DAMNED, Havocs, Legionaries, Possessed, Raptors, Warp Talons. HA may be up to half the army's points.
  • Enhancements: Fade to Darkness 30pts (kill in Fight phase + not in engagement → return to Strategic Reserves), Leaping Shadows 25pts (Scouts 9"). Both unchanged in 11th; both community "must-takes." Core combo: Chaos Lord + Legionaries + Fade, re-enter via Rapid Ingress (Lord's free-strat ability).
  • Stratagems: Binding Shadow 1CP (end of opp Fight phase, up to 1 HA + 1 daemon unit → Strategic Reserves); Channeled Wrath 1CP ([LANCE], +1 AP if Khorne); Shade Path NERFED in 11th: now start of opp Charge phase, visible enemy within 12", −1" charge (was −2).
  • 11th-ed environment changes:
  • Cover = −1 to hit (not save bonus) → army-wide buff for invuln-reliant Daemons; the single biggest reason Daemons are quietly good.
  • Hidden (rule 13.09): infantry/beasts/swarms in light/dense terrain that didn't shoot → detected only within 15"; Gone to Ground (13.11.01) drops that to 12".
  • One stratagem per unit per phase — constrains the Fade/Ingress/Binding toolkit.
  • Jul 22 2026 points: Lord of Change 300→320 (and 340 for 3rd+ — 11th has repeat-purchase scaling on some sheets), Bloodcrushers +10 (6-model), Kairos +10, Fluxmaster −10, Shalaxi −25.

Thralls of the First Prince — verbatim rule text

From https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/chaos-daemons/#Shadow-Legion (raw HTML, extracted 2026-07-27):

"When mustering your army, you cannot include any DAEMON PRINCE, DAEMON PRINCE WITH WINGS or EPIC HERO units (excluding BE'LAKOR), but you can include the following HERETIC ASTARTES units: CHAOS LORD, CHAOS LORD IN TERMINATOR ARMOUR, CHAOS LORD WITH JUMP PACK, CHAOS TERMINATOR SQUAD, CHOSEN, DAMNED units, Dark Apostle, HAVOCS, LEGIONARIES, Master of Possession, Possessed, RAPTORS, SORCERER, SORCERER IN TERMINATOR ARMOUR, WARP TALONS. The combined points value of such units depends on your battle size, as shown below. Incursion: Up to 500 pts / Strike Force: Up to 1000 pts / Onslaught: Up to 1500 pts."

Important clarification: the sentence "None of these models can be your WARLORD, and they cannot be given Enhancements" appears on the same Wahapedia page but belongs to the Daemonic Pact ally rule (daemons-into-CSM), NOT to Shadow Legion. Shadow Legion imposes no warlord or enhancement bar on its HA allies; instead it grants them the SHADOW LEGION keyword, which is the only gate on all four enhancements.

Epic Hero ban — implications

Confirmed EPIC HERO on datasheet keywords line (all illegal in Shadow Legion): Skulltaker, Karanak, Skarbrand, Kairos Fateweaver, Rotigus, Shalaxi Helbane, The Masque, Epidemius, The Changeling, The Blue Scribes, Syll'Esske. Confirmed NOT Epic Hero (all legal): Bloodthirster, Lord of Change, Great Unclean One, Keeper of Secrets, Skullmaster, Bloodmaster, Rendmaster, Fateskimmer, Fluxmaster, Exalted Flamer, Changecaster.

Skullmaster (Herald on Juggernaut) — keywords: MOUNTED, CHARACTER, CHAOS, DAEMON, KHORNE, SKULLMASTER, SHADOW LEGION. Leader: Bloodcrushers. This is the tournament replacement for Skulltaker.

Bloodthirster verdict

  • Legal in Shadow Legion: yes (SHADOW LEGION keyword native on datasheet; not Epic Hero).
  • Can take Fade to Darkness: yes. Enhancement text is "SHADOW LEGION model only" with no further gate; core rules 25.04 (Select Enhancements) says CHARACTER only, EPIC HERO never — no MONSTER exclusion exists. Adversarial pass CONFIRMED.
  • Points: 320 (335 for 3rd+). Great Axe loadout (7A S16 AP-4 D6+2 strike / 14A S10 AP-2 D2 sweep) is community consensus.
  • Community split: Rules-analysis (TTB Detachment Focus, 1d6chan, Warphammer 5-1 report) rates BT+Fade top-tier. TheChirurgeon's playtesting (same TTB article) prefers scouting Skullmaster+Bloodcrushers over a scouting BT because a T1-committed Bloodthirster often trades down. The Fade build sidesteps that concern. All 5/5 logged 4-1 Shadow Legion results on Listhammer since 11th launch run a Fadethirster.

Be'lakor — Supreme Commander

Datasheet ability text: "If this model is in your army, it must be your Warlord." Confirmed present on https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/chaos-daemons/Be-lakor. → In any list including Be'lakor, no other model may be Warlord.
